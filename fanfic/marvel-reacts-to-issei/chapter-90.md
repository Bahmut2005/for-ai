## THEATRE LEDGER — SESSION 90

**ENTRY 1: THE REQUEST**

The Theatre's voice manifested not as text but as vibration—a deep resonance that suggested the system itself was communicating rather than merely presenting.

**INSTRUCTION RECEIVED: THE SUBJECT MUST ENGAGE WITH THE GACHA DIRECTLY. THIS IS PROTOCOL. THIS IS NECESSITY. THE THEATRE DEMANDS PARTICIPATION.**

Everyone in the observation chamber went still.

"What does that mean?" Steve Rogers asked carefully.

"It means," Fury said quietly, "that the Theatre has been showing us footage of his life, but it hasn't actually involved him in the game. It's passive observation. Now it wants active participation."

Issei appeared in the chamber moments later, summoned by whatever force operated the Theatre. He looked at the massive gacha wheel, now transformed into something more—not just a display system, but what appeared to be an actual mechanical device, ancient and terrible.

"The system wants me to pull," Issei said, reading the intent more than the words. "To choose my own memory to expose."

"Or," Shuri said quietly, "to not choose. To see what the system brings up if you engage instead of being observed."

Issei approached the wheel slowly, deliberately. His wives were present now—Rias, Akeno, Xenovia, Irina, Asia, Koneko, Kuroka, and Rossweisse. They moved to surround him, not restraining but present. Supporting.

"Whatever happens," Rias said softly, "we're here."

Issei placed his hand on the wheel.

```
[GACHA PULL] — Initiated by Subject
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SYSTEM RECOGNITION: ADMINISTRATOR CLASS
AUTHORIZATION: CONFIRMED
PULLING FROM: RESTRICTED ARCHIVES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[SECRET-CLASS] — MEMORY REMOVED BY SUBJECT
```

The wheel spun, ancient machinery grinding as if it hadn't moved in centuries. The sound was wrong—not the pleasant chime of previous pulls, but something discordant, like metal protesting.

It stopped.

The result appeared in letters that burned like acid on reality itself:

**MEMORY SEALED BY ISSEI HYOUDOU — CLASSIFIED AT HIGHEST TIER**

```
[SYSTEM STATUS] — ATTEMPTING RECALL
> Archive Integrity: CONFIRMED
> Seal Strength: MAXIMUM
> Reason for Seal: PROTECTION AGAINST [REDACTED]
> Seal Duration: PERMANENT UNLESS AUTHORIZED REMOVAL
```

Issei's eyes went white—not with power, not with transformation, but with memory. The Theatre itself seemed to hold its breath as data that was never meant to be seen began to manifest.

The vision appeared in fragments, like a recording corrupted and partially deleted:

*A child. Younger than canonical Issei. Crying. In a place that wasn't the DxD world—the architecture wrong, the sky wrong, the very air tasting of something else.*

*A voice—unmistakably Issei's, but deeper, older, carrying the weight of exhaustion beyond measure:*

*"I'm sorry. I'm sorry. You won't remember this. I made sure of it. I made sure none of this reaches that timeline, that that version of you never has to carry this weight."*

*Then—a presence. Massive. Hungry. Made of *nothing*. Made of erased possibilities. Made of all the futures that died so that other futures could live.*

*Issei, older still—in a form the Theatre had never shown, scarred in ways that suggested survival of things that should not survive—standing against it.*

*And a decision. A terrible, beautiful decision.*

*"I am removing this. All of it. Every moment. Every timeline where you touched us. I am erasing you from my memory because if you have a pathway through my consciousness, you can use it to reach them. So I forget. So I protect."*

The memory fractured and dispersed, dissolved back into whatever archive it had been sealed in.

```
[THEATRE - ABSOLUTE SILENCE]
> Cosmic Entity Response Time: FROZEN
> Living Tribunal: NON-RESPONSIVE
> Galactus: MOTIONLESS
> Thanos: STILL
```

Issei's hand fell away from the wheel. His wives rushed forward, but they could see something had fundamentally changed in that moment. He'd looked into his own sealed memory and stared down something that the Theatre—the all-seeing Theatre—had never even shown them.

Something that had forced him to erase his own consciousness to protect them.

---

**ENTRY 2: THE TRUTH AND ITS COST**

"Tell us," Fury said quietly. "All of it. What was that?"

Issei turned, and his eyes were normal again, but there was something behind them now. Recognition. Understanding. Ancient knowledge that he'd lived with but never let conscious memory fully grasp.

"The Devourer," he said quietly. "The thing that's been coming since the beginning. The thing that eats timelines. The thing that isn't conquering or destroying anything—it's *devouring* them. Taking them. Making them part of itself."

"And you've fought it before," Rias said. It wasn't a question. She knew her husband. She knew the shape of his scars.

"I've fought it in futures I don't remember," Issei confirmed. "Timelines where I was older, where I had more power, where it still wasn't enough. And I learned something: it can follow consciousness. It can trace attachment points. So I learned to break the connection. To seal away entire memories, entire battles, entire versions of myself."

"That's why you're here," T'Challa said slowly, understanding dawning. "The Theatre pulled you because you're the connection point to something that exists *outside* normal multiversal structure."

"Not just outside," Issei said. "The Devourer exists in the spaces between timelines. In the gaps. In the possibilities that died. It's made of *failure*. Made of every timeline that collapsed. Every universe that fell. Every version of every person who couldn't survive."

Tony Stark's voice was very small: "And you fought it by yourself?"

"In those futures, yes," Issei said. "And I lost. Every version of me, in every timeline where I remembered and fought, I lost. So I learned a different strategy: forget. Protect. Make sure the ones I love don't carry the weight of knowing what was coming."

"But now you remember," Doom said from the shadows where he'd been observing. "The Theatre forced you to remember."

"Not forced," Issei corrected. "Allowed. The Theatre didn't force the memory. It just showed me what I'd sealed away. I chose to look. I chose to remember."

"Why?" Magneto asked, and there was something almost gentle in his voice—the voice of someone who'd spent his life fighting for causes he believed in and recognized that same commitment in another.

"Because if I'm here," Issei said, "if the Theatre brought me to Marvel, then this version of me—the one that doesn't remember—has a chance the others didn't. And that chance means I need to know what I'm protecting against. I need to remember why I chose to forget."

```
[THEATRE - SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]
> Arc Nine Complete
> Arc Ten Initialization: IMMINENT
> Subject Danger Classification: REVISED UPWARD
> Audience Understanding: FUNDAMENTALLY ALTERED
```

**ENTRY 3: THE WEIGHT OF SEALED KNOWLEDGE**

Rias sat beside Issei, not saying anything, but simply present. Akeno held his other hand. Koneko leaned against his shoulder in her characteristic way of offering comfort without drama.

"There's something worse," Issei said quietly, and no one interrupted him because they could all feel it. The thing that hadn't appeared in the memory but was implied in every word he'd spoken.

"In those futures I don't remember," he continued, "there were children. My children. Different ones than I have now—timelines branch and change after all. And I... I had to make choices that erased them to protect the current timeline. I had to accept that entire versions of people I love would never exist so that you all could."

The weight of that statement pressed down on the entire chamber.

"You can't let that be the only reason you remember," Natasha said suddenly. Her voice was quiet but urgent. "You can't carry that weight and nothing else. It will destroy you."

"I know," Issei said. "But it's the truth. And if the Theatre forced me to remember the truth about myself, then I need to accept all of it. The sacrifices. The futures erased. The versions of me that failed. All of it."

"Then why," Wanda asked, "are you sitting here talking to us instead of in a room trying not to fall apart?"

"Because," Issei said, and there was the faintest hint of a smile, "I learned a long time ago that the weight is easier if people are there to help carry it. Even the weight of remembering horrible things. Even the weight of knowing what's coming."

---

**ENTRY 4: THE FINAL MESSAGE**

The Theatre's voice manifested one more time:

**ARC NINE CONCLUDES.**

**THE SUBJECT HAS SEEN HIMSELF. THE AUDIENCE HAS SEEN THE SUBJECT. THE TRUTH HAS BEGUN TO SURFACE.**

**ARC TEN BEGINS WITH A QUESTION THAT REQUIRES AN ANSWER:**

**WHAT DO YOU DO WHEN YOU REMEMBER THAT YOU DELIBERATELY FORGOT?**

**AND WHAT DO YOU DO WHEN YOU DISCOVER THAT THE THING YOU WERE PROTECTING AGAINST IS STILL COMING?**

**THE REMAINING TEN CHAPTERS WILL SHOW YOU THE ANSWER.**

**THEY WILL SHOW YOU WHAT A DRAGON DOES WHEN HE REMEMBERS HE IS NOT JUST A PROTECTOR, BUT A SOLDIER IN A WAR THAT STRETCHES ACROSS ALL POSSIBLE TIME.**

**AND THEY WILL SHOW YOU WHETHER THAT SOLDIER CAN FIND REASONS TO KEEP FIGHTING BEYOND SURVIVAL.**

**BECAUSE SURVIVAL IS NOT A REASON. SURVIVAL IS A CONSEQUENCE.**

**THE REASON IS WHAT YOU DO WITH THE TIME YOU'RE GIVEN.**

**PREPARE FOR ARC TEN.**

**PREPARE FOR THE DEVOURER.**

**PREPARE FOR THE TRUTH OF WHAT ISSEI HYOUDOU TRULY IS.**

The lights dimmed. The gacha wheel stilled.

In the silence, Issei held his wives close, and they held him, and in that moment, the entire theatre understood something fundamental:

The power had never been the point.

The reason had always been love.

And love, unfortunately, was not going to be enough against something made of devoured worlds and forgotten futures.

But they would try anyway.

- Issei has fought the Devourer across multiple timeline iterations and lost every engagement—only surviving through strategic memory erasure.
- The existence of present-timeline Issei and his family required the deliberate erasure of alternate-timeline versions of his loved ones.
- The Theatre system recognized Issei's administrator-level access, suggesting he is something far more than a simple dragon emperor.
- What appeared to be random gacha pulls across 90 chapters were actually systematic exposure of Issei's nature to prepare the Marvel multiverse for what is coming.

- All factional divisions become secondary to the revelation that a multiversal threat exists that requires cooperation to combat.
- Doom, Magneto, Thanos, and other antagonists now understand they are observing someone with foreknowledge of a threat that dwarfs their own conflicts.

- The wives' role shifts from romantic/family support to warrior-partners aware they are in a war that stretches across all possible futures.

## FINAL SESSION NOTATION

**Arc 9 Complete: "Marvel Meets Issei"**

Over the course of this arc, the Theatre showed Marvel a dragon emperor navigating meetings, technical collaborations, philosophical questions about responsibility and fatherhood, recruitment attempts from villains, and finally, revelation of a cosmic threat that rewrites the entire narrative.

Issei is no longer simply powerful. He is now revealed as someone carrying knowledge—and burden—that transcends normal hero-villain categorization. He is a soldier aware that the war he fights extends beyond this timeline, beyond this version of himself, into the vast erasure of all possible futures.

Arc Ten will show what that soldier does with that knowledge.

What that husband does with that burden.

What that emperor does when he remembers why he was fighting in the first place.

And whether love, connection, and community can stand against something made of the complete opposite: isolation, consumption, and the erasure of all possibility.
