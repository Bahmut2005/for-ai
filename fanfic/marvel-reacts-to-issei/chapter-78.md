## ⚔ THEATRE LEDGER: THE WAR BEGINS

**[HUD: DIMENSIONAL BARRIER—ACTIVE | SEAL FORMATIONS—42% INTEGRITY | CASUALTIES—CALCULATING...]**

The sky splits open, but it's not the sky anymore. It's the boundary between worlds, ripping itself into a wound that bleeds darkness and divine fury. Trihexa—the Beast of Apocalypse—doesn't walk into existence. It *tears* into it, and the fabric of multiple realities screams.

In the observation chamber, high above the battlefield sprawling across sacred and profane ground alike, the Avengers watch through reinforced magical windows. Tony Stark's holographic displays multiply frantically, trying to catalog an entity that defies every database in his arsenal.

"That thing," Wanda whispers, her hands trembling, "I can't— there's no mind to touch. It's like trying to read a hurricane."

Steve Rogers stands rigid, jaw clenched. He's faced gods and aliens and the very concept of extinction. This is different. This moves with *purpose* that transcends malice. It's not evil—it's entropy made manifest, and no shield can stop entropy.

Nearby, Thor's fists are clenched so hard his knuckles bleed gold. "The boy," he rumbles. "Hyoudou. He's—"

"Still standing," Black Widow interrupts, though her voice wavers. "Barely."

Issei Hyoudou is not a figure in this moment. He is a silhouette against the end of everything, a red glow in the darkness, hovering at the edge of what should be his limit and what necessity demands he exceed.

---

**[SEALING TEAM ALPHA—COORDINATE: EASTERN PILLAR | STATUS: ENGAGED]**

Kiba Yuuto moves like water through broken light, his holy demonic sword leaving trails of white and red. Around him, the sealing team—dozens of devils and fallen angels, reincarnated humans and allied supernatural species—chants in languages that predate kingdoms. Their magic builds walls that the Beast immediately begins to dismantle.

"The formation is fracturing!" someone screams into the comm.

"Hold it!" Akeno's voice cuts through, commanding and ruthless. She's not the cheerful girl anymore. War has stripped that away. Lightning cascades from her fingertips in organized, devastating patterns. Each bolt targets specific nodes in Trihexa's presence, trying to contain something that should not exist in a single dimensional space. "Rias!"

The Gremory heiress, standing at the nexus point of the eastern seal, pours everything into the ritual. Blood runs from her nose, her ears. Her power is infinite by normal standards. Against Trihexa, she might as well be offering droplets to quench a volcano.

"Sealing success rate for Eastern Pillar: 19%," a tactical voice announces, emotionless and damning.

Somewhere in the chaos, a devil screams. Then another. The Beast's attention focuses there like a lens. What it looks at stops existing.

---

In the reaction chamber, Magneto watches without expression, but his hands betray him—metal objects around him vibrate with suppressed rage and fear.

"Children," he says quietly. "They're just children."

"Some of them are immortal," someone corrects.

"That's not the point," Erik responds. "The point is they shouldn't have to—" He stops himself. He's watched revolutions. He's seen wars. But there's something about the sheer *scale* of this, the resignation in the way these fighters prepare for positions they know might be temporary.

---

**[PHASE TWO: ISSEI ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOL—INITIALIZING]**

Issei doesn't feel like himself anymore. That stopped being an option three days ago.

His Balance Breaker form is just a starting point now. The red armor that once felt like infinite power is a foundation. On top of it, the gifts of the Infinity Dragons layer like psychological weight made manifest. Ophis's power makes him feel hollow—*powerful*, but the sensation of self is dulled, like he's wearing someone else's body. Great Red's draconic fury burns hot, pushing him toward aggression that has to be carefully modulated or he'll destroy his own allies.

But none of that matters. Only the seals matter. Only buying time.

"Ddraig," he calls out, his voice split between human and beast. The Welsh Dragon responds from within him, and for a moment, they are one entity.

Trihexa turns its attention to him.

It's not like being looked at. It's like being unmade by a glance. Issei feels his power armor strain, feels the blessing gifts flare defensively, feels the part of his soul that isn't protected by any dragon scream.

He moves forward anyway.

The first blow shatters his gauntlet. The second burns through seals on his shoulder. By the seventh, he's bleeding from places that shouldn't exist, wounds that violate physical geometry.

"Issei!" Rias's voice, but he can't respond. He has to keep moving. He has to give the northern seal team another three minutes. The eastern team is already falling apart.

---

In the observation chamber, several Avengers look away. Only Steve, Wanda, and Thor keep watching.

"That kid's dying," a mutant operative whispers.

"He's already dead," another corrects. "He just doesn't know it yet. None of this generation does. They're corpses walking through war."

Steve thinks about all the wars he's fought. All the teams. All the sacrifices. None of them prepared him for watching someone young and fragile and *trying so hard* get broken in real-time.

"No," he says quietly. "He's not dead yet. That's the whole point. None of them are. Not until it's actually over."

---

**[SEALING TEAM DELTA—APOPHIS CONTAINMENT | CRITICAL STATUS]**

The evil dragons have to be handled simultaneously with Trihexa. This is the cruel mathematics of the apocalypse: you cannot focus all your power on one threat when the universe provides multiple extinction events on the same schedule.

Apophis is older than most civilizations. Older than some religions. It is also, crucially, *stupid*—or at least, its intelligence is alien and inflexible. Where Trihexa operates on principles that transcend morality and logic, Apophis is purely predatory instinct compressed into a cosmic body.

Issei's second form, the one he never wanted to use, is what was created to fight this specific beast.

He separates himself from his own consciousness for a moment, lets the gifts take over in a controlled manner that Sirzechs claims is theoretically impossible. For maybe ten seconds, the thing wearing Issei's body is something else entirely. It's a counter-dragon, a purpose-built destroyer, and it matches Apophis blow for blow.

Apophis dies.

Or it's sealed so completely that death becomes an academic distinction.

When Issei regains cognitive control, he's on his knees, and part of his chest cavity isn't properly anchored to his body anymore. He can see his own ribs. He can see through them to a space that isn't quite empty but isn't quite full either.

The price of using gifts meant for entities beyond his capacity to house.

"No time," he mutters, and pushes forward toward the central seal. "No time to die."

---

**[HUD: CENTRAL SEAL—ACTIVATION | CONVERGENCE PATTERN: 67% | BEAST RESISTANCE: INCREASING]**

The final seal requires all of them: the surviving sealing teams (casualties mounting through every minute), the allied forces from the supernatural factions, the borrowed power from every dragon willing to contribute, and Issei at the absolute center, holding the pattern together through sheer force of will and a power source that's killing him by degrees.

Rias stands at the secondary node, blood streaming down her face. She's given so much power that her regeneration can't keep pace. Akeno is barely conscious, supported by Kiba. The fallen angel Azazel has stopped speaking—he's committed everything to the spell, and the human body has limits, even for his species.

Around them, dragons and devils chant the sealing words. Humans and supernatural beings alike pour their power into the pattern. The cost multiplies with every second.

A devil captain stops moving. His power goes dark. He's given everything his body could produce and then some. They don't stop chanting. They can't afford to stop.

An old fallen angel collapses. She's been alive for millennia, and she chooses to spend the last of her existence in this moment, in this seal. They carry her with them, her power still feeding into the pattern as her body shuts down.

A dragon—one of the lesser wyverns, not a true dragon but still mighty in its own right—doesn't have the power to get back up once it falls. It gives what it can from the ground, burning itself out.

The seal grows stronger.

---

In the reaction chamber, silence has become absolute. No one comments. No one moves. They just watch as an entire generation of warriors is spent like currency, and the currency is their own existence.

"How many?" someone finally asks.

No one answers because the count keeps climbing.

---

**[PHASE FINAL: CONTAINMENT ACHIEVED | CASUALTIES: 347 COMBATANTS | STATUS: HOLDING]**

It's not victory. Everyone who survives knows this.

The seal holds Trihexa down, but it's not permanent. It cannot be. The Beast is beyond permanence. The seal is a prison that will eventually fail. What the defenders have done is buy time—maybe years, maybe decades, maybe centuries if the seals are maintained. But eventually, Trihexa will return, or something equally terrible will emerge from whatever cosmic spaces breed such horrors.

Issei hovers at the center of the seal, barely conscious. His armor is destroyed. His wings are half-formed, flickering like they might dissipate if he stops concentrating. His body is held together by power and stubbornness and the gifts of dragons that are slowly consuming his spirit like a healing wound that eats the flesh.

Rias reaches for him, but Akeno stops her. "If you break the seal now—"

"I know," Rias whispers. She keeps her hands out, trembling in the air, useless.

Issei sees her, through vision that's getting darker at the edges. He tries to smile. It probably looks like a grimace of pain.

"We won," he says. It's a lie, but it's the kind of lie that soldiers tell because the alternative is breaking.

"You did it," Rias calls back, her voice shattered.

---

**[HUD: SEAL STATUS—HOLDING AT 96% INTEGRITY | COMBAT DURATION: 4 HOURS 23 MINUTES | SURVIVORS: APPROXIMATELY 2,847 COMBATANTS | WOUNDED: APPROXIMATELY 1,204 | CASUALTIES: APPROXIMATELY 347]**

In the reaction chamber, Steve Rogers finally steps away from the window.

"That's war," he says, and no one responds because there's nothing to say.

The Avengers, the mutants, the gods, the cosmic beings—they've all seen battle. But they've also seen this: the moment when victory and extinction become indistinguishable, when the only difference between winning and losing is how many people you have left to rebuild with.

Around them, others sit in stunned silence, watching the medical teams emerge from containment, seeing the stretchers, the careful collection of the dead, the ritual that comes after battle.

Somewhere in the chaos, a wife waits for news that her husband is alive.

Somewhere else, a friend learns that their companion didn't make it.

And at the center of it all, in the embrace of a seal that he cannot leave without destroying everything he just saved, Issei Hyoudou closes his eyes and doesn't allow himself to break.

Not yet.

Not while the seal needs him.
