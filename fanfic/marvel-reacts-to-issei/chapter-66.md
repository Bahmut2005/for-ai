# Chapter Sixty-Six: Galactus Looks Back

**Primary Reaction Groups:** Galactus • Silver Surfer • Cosmic Elders

**Gacha Pulls:**
1. "Galactus's Memory of a Destroyed Universe" — **Secret**
2. "Unidentified Draconic Signature" — **Mythic**

---

The theatre had not, in its entire operational existence, ever paused for Galactus to speak.

It was not disrespect — the World-Devourer held no opinion about respect. It was something more factual: when Galactus was present, Galactus simply *was*, the way mountains are when you enter a room built into their slope. The theatre's architecture seemed, in those intervals, to be holding its breath. The cosmic tier dropped lower in their seats. The Living Tribunal's bookmark glowed less frequently. And Galactus occupied the space above the stage like a thought occupying a mind: not moving, not still, but present in a way that made the distinction obsolete.

It had been sixty-five chapters of this accommodation.

Now, as the wheel assembled and the screen prepared to print, Galactus did something unprecedented. It *leaned forward*.

The entire mezzanine — several thousand beings who had never truly considered that the World-Devourer could move, not really, not in a space this small — felt the weight-shift in their marrow. The Silver Surfer's head came up. The Cosmic Elders exchanged glances that meant *do you feel that* and *yes* and *I do not like this*. The screen printed:

**GACHA PULL: 1 OF 2.**

The wheel did not spin. It *assembled*, piece by piece, the colors building outward from a core of deep, space-black — a hue no one had yet seen it use, the color of the void *before* stars, and the theatre flooded upward with that color to its highest reaches, blotting out the painted sky, and the screen stamped:

**"GALACTUS'S MEMORY OF A DESTROYED UNIVERSE" — RARITY: SECRET.**

"Secret tier," someone whispered. "We don't have—"

"We do now," said the Silver Surfer quietly, and every head in the cosmic section turned, because they had *never* heard that particular register in his voice. It was the register of something remembering why it was afraid.

**[THE AEONS REMEMBER WHAT WAS FORGOTTEN,]** said the screen, and the world fell away.

---

Before time had learned to stick in a line.

Galactus remembers this — not as memory works for younger things, but the way a mountain remembers stone: as the simple fact of its own existence embedded in everything it touches. There was a universe here. Not the one Issei Hyoudou walks through now. Not the one Marvel does. A third thing, or a hundredth thing, or a thousandth. Galactus devoured galaxies in the childhood of existence and has lost count of which universe they belonged to. The count stops mattering after a while.

This one had tasted of... copper and intelligence. The Elders would have called it beautiful. The Elders were young then, and beauty meant something. To Galactus, beauty and extinction are the same metric — it only means there is something worth consuming.

*But something was wrong here.*

The wrongness arrived as a disturbance in the cosmic fabric — a *signature*, the way a scent arrives before the wind. Draconic. Vast. And older than the universe it was tearing through.

Galactus watched it crest, in that space-before-time, and felt something it had rarely felt even in the aeons of its existence: *recognition*.

There was a dragon in this universe. Not small-made, not demon-born, not bound by the rules of divinity or flesh as Galactus understands them. This was something *else* — something that had learned to walk between the spaces where universes touch, and it had come here *looking for something*, or *running from something*, or *both*. The dragon was afraid. Galactus, who had made things fear itself as a foundational principle, had rarely seen fear in anything so large.

The dragon was also fighting.

But not the ways dragons fight. This was something else — something that looked like the universe *itself* was the enemy. The dragon's roar came out in cascades of impossible color, and reality *fractured* around the sound, and what Galactus perceived was the cosmic signature of a thing that should not exist, should not *persist*, could not exist in any universe that obeyed normal law.

What the dragon was fighting had no name. The theatre's vision showed Galactus's memory as Galactus experienced it: incomplete, fragmented, *wrong* in the way of prophecies that come too late. But the impression was clear. Something was eating the universe — not consuming it the way Galactus does, with the World-Devourer's ravenous appetite. This was consumption on another scale. This was the *erasing* of the universe itself, from the timeline, from the cosmic record, as if it had never existed at all.

And the dragon *lost*.

But Galactus remembers — and the memory is fragmentary, dreamlike, the texture of a doubt that wakes you in a dark room — that before the dragon lost, *it chose to lose*. In the final moment, when the cosmic signature showed the universe collapsing around them, the dragon stopped fighting and instead *held*, embracing the collapse, and the dragon and the erasure went down *together* into a space-between that Galactus could not follow.

The universe unraveled. Not slowly. Not in waves. In a single moment of *removal*, the entire cosmos — its stars, its histories, its people, everything — was taken out of the cosmic record, the way a page is torn from a book. When it was done, Galactus looked at the space where the universe had been and saw only void. Only the fact of absence.

Only the *certainty* that it had existed.

The dragon's roar echoed in the void for a long time after. Galactus listened to it. Galactus, who had devoured worlds and swallowed the light from galaxies, had never heard anything so absolute in its grief.

---

The vision released them.

The screen went dark. Not the gentle dark of a pulldown or a transition — the absolute dark of the void itself, held for three full seconds, and in those three seconds the theatre made no sound.

When the light came back, it came back pale. The cosmic tier was on their feet. The Living Tribunal was standing — and Galactus did not remember the Living Tribunal standing at all, not in the entire run of the theatre. The Cosmic Elders were conferring in the rapid-fire hush of beings who had lived through Creation Wars and were being reminded of why that experience had left scars.

Galactus still had not moved. But now its focus — the attention that was the weight of stars — had turned entirely inward.

"Galactus," said the Silver Surfer, and his voice had the gentle flatness of a man asking a question he does not want to hear the answer to. "In all your eons. Before today. Have you ever encountered that — that *signature*?"

For a moment, nothing. Then, and it seemed to take the aeons themselves to arrange it:

"**Once. Only once. A universe that was not destroyed, but unmade. A cosmos that the Cosmic Record lists as *Never-Was*, yet Galactus *remembers it was*. The contradiction was too profound to sustain. Galactus stopped trying to reconcile it, tens of thousands of years ago. The memory was considered, perhaps, a dream of the void.**"

"It wasn't a dream," said Uatu the Watcher, and his voice came from the highest tier, and it carried the weight of someone who does not forget. "Watchers do not dream. Watchers remember. The universe existed. I recorded it. And then it was *unmade* from all records except memory. As if someone reached back through time and removed it, while leaving the scars of its removal visible only to those old enough to see ghosts in the cosmic dust."

The screen chimed.

**GACHA PULL: 2 OF 2.**

The wheel built itself in careful silence. When it resolved, it landed on a color that seemed to *pulse* — the deep purple-gold that had so far only marked the Dragon Roar chapters, the draconic signature itself. And above it, the screen printed:

**"UNIDENTIFIED DRACONIC SIGNATURE" — RARITY: MYTHIC.**

**[COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS ENGAGED,]** said the theatre, in that voice that was not quite a voice at all, **[CROSS-REFERENCING COSMOLOGICAL SIGNATURES. PLEASE STAND BY.]**

The screen divided. On one side, the draconic signature of Issei Hyoudou — the now-familiar color-pattern of his cosmic nature, the frequency his existence sends out into the universe like sonar. On the other side, the draconic signature from Galactus's broken memory, fainter, older, resonating in octaves that hurt to perceive.

**[ANALYSIS COMPLETE.]**

**[RESULT: DRACONIC RESONANCE FREQUENCY DETECTED IN BOTH ISSEI HYOUDOU AND ERASED UNIVERSAL SIGNATURE. RESONANCE PATTERN: RELATED COSMOLOGIES OR TIMELINE ECHO. PROBABILITY OF DIRECT CONNECTION: INCALCULABLE. PROBABILITY THAT SIGNATURES ORIGINATE FROM SHARED SOURCE: 79.3%.]**

**[SUBSIDIARY FINDING: THEATRICAL ARCHITECTURE SCAN INITIATED ON DISCOVERED PARAMETERS.]**

**[RESULT: SIGNATURE BEHIND THEATRICAL SCREEN MATCHES DRACONIC RESONANCE ON BOTH FREQUENCIES — ISSEI HYOUDOU AND ERASED UNIVERSAL SIGNATURE.]**

**[FINDING: THEATRICAL ARCHITECT — ORIGIN UNKNOWN. FUNCTION: GUARDIAN/WITNESS/CUSTODIAN CLASS. DRACONIC ORIGIN — CONFIRMED.]**

**[SECONDARY FINDING: THEATRE BROUGHT MARVEL COSMOLOGY INTO PROXIMITY WITH ISSEI HYOUDOU. POSSIBLE PURPOSE: PREPARATION FOR COSMOLOGICAL THREAT OF ERASED UNIVERSAL SCALE.]**

The screen held those words for a full ten seconds. When the room finally understood what it was reading, the sound it made was the sound of a room comprehending that the ground under its feet was not ground at all, but very old water holding a specific weight.

"The theatre," Steve Rogers said slowly, "is run by a *dragon*."

"More than that," said Doom, and his voice had gone very quiet. "The theatre was *built* by a dragon. Built to prepare us. Built to bring us here to watch a boy learn to be *like* that. The dragon that was erased." A pause. The mask tilted. "The theatre did not choose to let us see Issei. The theatre chose to make us *ready* to survive what comes after."

"What destroyed the universe," the Living Tribunal said flatly, "is still out there."

"If it comes here," Silver Surfer began, and his voice was steady, but there was something underneath the steadiness that made everything in the cosmic tier brace itself, "then we have ten chapters of watching a boy learn discipline because—"

"Because he needs it," Galactus said, and the voice was absolute, measured, the sound of something that had already incorporated this into its understanding of existence. **"If that thing returns to this universe, Marvel will need every edge. Every discipline. Every mercy offered and every power earned. Ddraig has already wrapped himself around one champion. But Galactus is older than Ddraig. Galactus has stood before the Hunger Eternal and lived. Galactus will stand with the boy, if it comes to that. Because the alternative is erasure, and Galactus does not forget what was erased."**

The screen continued to print, even as the room processed this.

**[THEATRE PROTOCOL UPDATED: CHAPTERS 1-65 CLASSIFIED AS BASELINE PREPARATION. CHAPTERS 66-100 CLASSIFIED AS ACCLIMATION PROTOCOL FOR CIVILIZATION-THREAT-LEVEL COMBAT ENGAGEMENT. NEXT CHAPTER WILL INTRODUCE CELESTIAL-TIER ARTIFACTS FROM ISSEI HYOUDOU'S PERSONAL COLLECTION. PREPARATION RECOMMENDED.]**

"Celestial artifacts," Natasha said, very quietly. "In the vault of a seventeen-year-old from Japan."

"Not his," said Wong, and every sorcerer in the room heard the weight in it. "Those are *gifts*. Those are *payments*. Issei's vault contains proofs that beings of immense power have already encountered him and deemed it necessary to leave him prepared."

The cosmic elders conferred again, and this time the hush had the texture of consensus being reached about something terrible and necessary.

Galactus did not move. But the World-Devourer was, for the first time, *waiting*. Waiting for the next chapter. Waiting to see what god had been kind enough to arm the boy who had already fought, without knowing it, against the memory of something that erases universes.

The screen held its final line for a very long moment:

**[THE STAGE IS BEING SET FOR SOMETHING LARGER THAN ANY SINGLE COSMOS HAS HELD BEFORE. AUDIENCE PREPARATION: MANDATORY.]**

Then, in small script below:

**[ARC SEVEN: COSMIC PREPARATION. CHAPTER 66 OF 100: GALACTUS LOOKS BACK. NEXT: THE CELESTIAL VAULT.]**
