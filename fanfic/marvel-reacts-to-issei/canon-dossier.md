# Canon Reference: Issei Hyoudou (High School DxD)

Condensed from the source dossier used to plan this fic. Light novels are the
controlling canon; anime/manga/wiki are secondary and used only where they don't
contradict the novels.

## Power progression (in order unlocked)

1. **Boosted Gear** (base) — Longinus Sacred Gear housing Ddraig, the Welsh Dragon
   Emperor. `Boost` doubles the wielder's power every ten seconds, no theoretical
   cap (limited by the user's body). `Transfer` redistributes accumulated power to
   allies/objects. Later: `Penetrate` bypasses defenses, strikes an opponent's core.
2. **Boosted Gear: Scale Mail** — Balance Breaker. Red dragon armor, unrestricted
   boosting, later full wings/claws. Basic blast can erase a mountain.
3. **Illegal Move Triaina** — piece-based split specialization: Welsh Sonic Boost
   Knight (speed), Welsh Dragonic Rook (power/defense), Welsh Blaster Bishop
   (artillery, Dragon Blaster — can warp space-time briefly).
4. **Cardinal Crimson Promotion** ("True Queen" armor, alias C×C) — integrates all
   three Triaina forms. Solid Impact Booster, Crimson Blaster, Dividing Wyvern
   Fairies, Longinus Smasher. Reaches Satan-class opponents.
5. **Juggernaut Drive** — forbidden unsealed berserk state. Catastrophic: sanity
   loss, lifespan drain, curse-state. Not a stable combat mode.
6. **Super Gigantic Balance Breaker** — giant kaiju-scale fusion with Great Red.
7. **Diabolos Dragon God** — crimson-black armor powered by Ophis's infinity, four
   wings, Infinity Blaster. Ignores Rizevim's Sacred Gear Canceller. Original form
   risks disintegrating Issei's own existence.
8. **Pseudo Dragon Deification** — safer Diabolos derivative. Originally seconds to
   minutes; extended to 88 minutes via simplified Amrita. Fights God-class beings
   (Vidar, Nyx/Erebus-tier).
9. **True Dragon Deification** — Great Red's flesh resonates fully. Defeats Angra
   Mainyu, joins the fight against Hades. Issei recognized as a Super Devil.
10. **Apocalypse AnswerArms (AxA)** — fusion with familiar Ryuuteimaru, taps Great
    Red's aura. Revelation Barrier, multiple Apocalypse Ascalon blades, all-range
    blasters. Can destroy a planet in one shot if misused; leaves Issei and
    Ryuuteimaru exhausted for days.

**Terminology note:** no official English source standardizes "BxB." Use
*Boosted Gear: Scale Mail / Balance Breaker* in narration unless deliberately
using fandom shorthand.

- **Ascalon II** — additional summoned sword in later states.

## Psychology

Loudmouthed pervert, dreams of being "Harem King," academically mediocre — and
also: kind, self-sacrificing, principled, resistant to the corruption pattern of
prior Red Dragon Emperors. Refuses to exploit emotionally vulnerable women even
when willing. Rarely kills; prefers arrest/defeat to execution; forgives former
enemies (Riser, Kuroka, Vali, Cao Cao). Carries lasting fear of romantic rejection
from Raynare's betrayal and murder.
