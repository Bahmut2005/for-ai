# Chapter Seventy-Two: The Smile After Trauma

**Canon Status:** [DRAMATIZATION] Built around the canon Kokabiel incident (the fallen angel's assault on Kuoh Academy, the theft of holy swords, Asia and Xenovia's near-deaths); reaction framing and interior perspective are fic invention.

**Primary Reaction Groups:** Trauma Survivors (Natasha Romanoff, Bucky Barnes, Logan Howlett, Jessica Jones) • Deadpool • Robert Reynolds (Sentry)

**Gacha Pulls:**
1. "Humour in the Face of Pain" — **Rare**
2. "Private Breakdown" — **Secret**

---

The theatre had gone quiet in the particular way it did after a pull that promised to hurt. Not the reverent hush of a power reveal. A held breath.

**[GACHA SYSTEM: PULL SEQUENCE INITIATED]**
**[PULL 1: "HUMOUR IN THE FACE OF PAIN" — RARE — ACTIVATING]**

The screen didn't show a battlefield. It showed a courtyard, scorched, one wall of the old school building caved into rubble, the sky over Kuoh still faintly bruised with the afterglow of a fallen angel's power. Kokabiel was gone—sealed, driven off, however the theatre would eventually explain it—but the cost of the hour was written across everyone still standing.

Issei stood at the center of it, armor cracked at the shoulder, one arm held at an angle that suggested a dislocation nobody had set yet. Blood, not all of it his, dried along his jaw. Behind him, the club building smoldered. Beyond that, the line where Xenovia and Asia had nearly died was still cordoned by broken practice swords and a crater that hadn't finished cooling.

Rias was mid-sentence, checking him with the particular fury of someone who'd almost lost a piece she couldn't replace. "—you took a direct hit, I don't care what your Sacred Gear tells you, sit down and let Asia—"

Issei raised his good hand.

"Before anyone panics," he said, and his voice came out bright, almost musical in its wrongness given the wreckage around him, "yes, I'm fine. No, I don't need to lie down. And yes, I'm aware I got thrown through a wall *and* stabbed in the same afternoon, which I think technically qualifies me for hazard pay, if Devils did that."

Nobody in the courtyard laughed. Koneko's ears were flat. Kiba's smile didn't reach his eyes.

"That's not funny," Xenovia said quietly, her own wounds still weeping under makeshift bandages.

"It's a little funny," Issei said. The smile didn't move.

In the theatre, the reaction was immediate and specific, the way a struck bell is specific.

Natasha Romanoff didn't laugh either. She watched the screen with the stillness of someone recognizing her own reflection in a window she hadn't meant to look through. "He's scared," she said, to no one in particular. "The jokes mean he's scared."

Bucky's jaw worked. "I know that mechanism. Steve did it after Zola's factory. Cracked jokes for three days before he let anyone near him with a look at his ribs."

Logan didn't say anything for a moment, just watched the boy on-screen keep his hand raised, keep his voice light, keep performing *fine* for an audience of people who loved him too much to be fooled by it. "Kid do anything reckless in there?" he finally asked.

**[PULL 2: "PRIVATE BREAKDOWN" — SECRET — ACTIVATING]**

The vision didn't wait for an answer. It cut—the theatre's gacha system had never been gentle about sequencing—to hours later. Night. The Hyoudou house, quiet, everyone else finally asleep or pretending to be. Issei sat alone on the edge of his bed, Boosted Gear dormant on his wrist, hands very still in his lap.

Then not still.

"I keep seeing it," he said, to nobody, to the dark, to Ddraig maybe, though the dragon didn't answer this time. "The light spear. Going through Asia. I got there— I got there in *time*, she's fine, she's *fine*, but for about two seconds I watched it happen and I couldn't move fast enough and—"

His hands had started shaking. He pressed them flat against his knees like that would stop it.

"I don't get to fall apart," he said. "Rias needs me steady. Asia needs me steady. If I fall apart now I don't get to put myself back together before the next thing finds us, and there's always a next thing."

He didn't cry. He'd apparently decided, somewhere along the way, that this wasn't a thing he did anymore. Instead his jaw worked, and his breathing went ragged and controlled and ragged again, three times, before he lay back on the bed still fully dressed, staring at the ceiling like it owed him an explanation.

**Robert Reynolds** watched this in absolute silence, and when he finally spoke, his voice carried the particular weight of a man who understood the shape of a mind trying to contain something enormous.

"He's broadcasting," Sentry said. "Not telepathically—I don't think he even knows how loud he is. But I can feel the shape of it from here. Pressure with nowhere to go. That's not strength. That's a dam."

"Everybody in this room knows what happens to dams," Jessica Jones said flatly.

Deadpool, for once, wasn't laughing. He was watching with something that might, on him, have passed for genuine unease. "Kid's got the bit down cold, though. The self-deprecating hero joke, timed perfect, delivered with a smile that doesn't move his eyes. That's not comedy. That's *training*. Somebody—something—taught him that jokes buy silence, and silence buys time to hide."

"Nobody trained him to do that," Natasha said. "That's the tragedy of it. Nobody had to. He just needed to survive being loved by people he was terrified of losing, and this is what his body invented to make that survivable."

Bucky leaned forward, elbows on his knees. "It works. That's the worst part. It works right up until the day it doesn't."

---

The theatre held the image a long moment: Issei on his back, eyes open, breathing counted out like a soldier rationing air.

**"OBSERVATION,"** the system intoned, and for once its voice carried something almost gentle. **"THE SUBJECT HAS NOT YET LEARNED THAT ASKING FOR HELP AND FAILING TO PROTECT ARE NOT THE SAME FAILURE. THIS LESSON WILL BE EXPENSIVE."**

Logan finally spoke again, quiet in the way that meant he'd stopped performing for the room. "Every one of us in this section has had a night like that one. Some of us had that night for decades before somebody made us stop performing fine." He looked at Natasha. At Bucky. At Jessica. "The kid's got people around him who love him. That's more than some of us had. Question is whether they're paying attention, or whether they're gonna let the smile fool them same as everybody else in that courtyard did."

"Asia noticed," Jessica said, nodding at a detail the theatre hadn't emphasized but the sharper eyes in the room had caught: in the courtyard footage, replayed a beat, Asia's hand had lingered on Issei's sleeve half a second longer than the injury required. "She wasn't checking the wound. She was checking *him*."

"Good," Sentry said. "That's the only variable that matters. Not whether he breaks. Whether somebody's close enough to notice when he does."
