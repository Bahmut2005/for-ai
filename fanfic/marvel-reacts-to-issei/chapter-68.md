## Theatre: The Cosmic Chamber

The theatre manifests as something almost incomprehensible. Not a room. Not a space. It is a *courtroom* that exists in the fold between universes, where the fabric of reality is thin enough to be transparent, yet sturdy enough to support judgment.

The walls are alive with data.

Streams of cosmic light cascade down in geometric patterns—the biographical records of every being who has ever mattered, ever threatened, ever *transcended*. They flow like rivers of starlight, each one encoding the essential nature of existence itself. Gods are represented by golden threads. Mortals shimmer in copper. Demons burn in violet fire. Abstracts are written in mathematics that shouldn't exist.

The Living Tribunal sits in the center.

It is not one entity, but three: Law, Justice, and Judgment, unified in purpose if not form. Each judge appears as a distinct presence, though their individual shapes are difficult to perceive. To look directly at them is to feel your mind struggle against the weight of cosmic authority. They have judged the birth and death of universes. They have weighed the fates of god-kings. They are the ultimate arbiters, the court of last resort for every being that ever walks between worlds.

Around them, the Watchers stand in concentric circles—ancient beings of impossible age, their eyes reflecting the light of a billion dead stars. They observe. They have *always* observed. They are here not to judge, but to bear witness, because some truths are only true if someone *sees* them.

And then there is Issei.

Not physically present—no mortal vessel could survive the cosmic pressure of this place—but *represented*. His essence floats in the center of the chamber like a star that refuses to collapse into a black hole. Around him, the classification matrix begins to spin.

## Gacha Pull 1: Classification Attempt by the Living Tribunal

**Rarity:** Secret

The Living Tribunal raises its collective will, and the matrix *BEGINS*.

It is not a gentle process. The system that categorizes beings across all existence is not built for mercy or subtlety. It is built for *clarity*. Every being must fit somewhere, because the multiverse cannot function if existence does not have order.

The judges lean forward in unison.

**[CLASSIFICATION PROTOCOL INITIATED]**
**[SUBJECT: ISSEI HYOUDOU]**
**[SCANNING COSMIC NATURE...]**

The first filter activates.

**MORTAL?**

The system examines Issei's frame, his origin. A human, born in the realm of the living, subject to death, subject to time. But then—

*No.*

Infinity crawls through his veins. He has inherited the power of the Ophis—the Ouroboros Dragon, the serpent who eats infinity itself. He carries the balance breaker known as Juggernaut Drive. He wields Sacred Gears that rewrite the rules of his own existence. He has shed his mortality like a snake sheds skin, again and again, each time emerging more than he was.

**RESULT: MORTAL.EXE — NULL**
**REASON: SUBJECT EXCEEDS MORTAL TAXONOMY BY FACTOR OF INFINITY**

**DEMON?**

Issei was killed by a fallen angel named Raynare and resurrected by a devil named Rias Gremory. He lives under devil law. He operates in the hierarchies of Hell. He commands devil power. His blood, after hundreds of evolutions, carries draconic devil essence. By every legal and magical metric, he *should* be classified as demon.

But.

He refuses the throne of Hell. He spurns divinity's offered seat. He stands alone, choosing comrades over court, bonds over birthright. The classification system screams that he is *of* demon, but he is not *bound* to demon. He walks between worlds as a ghost in their hierarchy.

**RESULT: DEMON.EXE — PARTIAL MATCH**
**WARNING: SUBJECT EXHIBITS NON-COMPLIANCE WITH DEMONOLOGICAL HIERARCHY**

**GOD?**

The Living Tribunal sees his power ascend. Issei achieves god-tier status across multiple iterations. His strength rivals celestials. His will shapes probability. His sacred gears grant him dominion over concepts themselves. He has killed gods. He has befriended gods. He has *become* god.

And then he refuses it.

The classification system processes this refusal, and something strange happens. The refusal *means something*. In a universe where power determines status, a being who achieves godhood and then *chooses not to accept it* creates a logical impossibility. You cannot classify someone as a god if they refuse the classification, because godhood is not merely power—it is *status*, and status requires acceptance.

**RESULT: DEITY.EXE — CONTRADICTORY DATA**
**ERROR: SUBJECT REFUSES DIVINE APPOINTMENT**
**CLASSIFICATION FAILED: REFUSAL OF STATUS = AUTOMATIC DISQUALIFICATION**

**ANOMALY?**

Yes. Obviously, yes. The system flags him as anomaly by default. But "anomaly" is a catch-all, a confession that the classifier does not understand. In the court of the Living Tribunal, "anomaly" is not a classification—it is a *failure of classification*. It is the cosmic equivalent of shrugging.

**RESULT: ANOMALY.EXE — CONFIRMED BUT INSUFFICIENT**

The system now begins to *panic*.

The classification matrix opens new pathways. It reaches deeper into its taxonomy, searching for subcategories that might contain him. The Living Tribunal has never encountered a being it cannot fit somewhere.

**Herald?**
*He carries messages and power from dragons, but he serves no sovereign. PARTIAL MATCH.*

**Cosmic Entity?**
*He possesses cosmic scope, but he does not transcend the laws of the multiverse. He bends them, but remains bound to consequence. INSUFFICIENT MATCH.*

**Draconic Sovereign?**
*He commands dragon power, but he rules no kingdom, holds no throne. FAILED MATCH.*

**Super Devil?**
*He has achieved this classification once, twice, multiple times. But he has also shed it, evolved past it, become something else. OUTDATED CLASSIFICATION.*

**Celestial-Kin?**
*He approaches celestial status, but he is not born of the cosmic forces. He is *forged* by them, tempered in their fire. CONTAMINATION ALERT.*

**Living Weapon?**
*He has been used as a weapon, will be used as a weapon again. But he refuses to be *merely* a weapon. He claims something beyond utility. CLASSIFICATION REJECTED BY SUBJECT.*

The matrix spins faster.

The classification system is built to never fail. Every being has a place. Every being fits *somewhere*.

But Issei—

**[CRITICAL ERROR]**
**[SUBJECT DEFIES CATEGORIZATION]**
**[MULTIPLE SIMULTANEOUS STATUSES DETECTED]**
**[INCOMPATIBLE DESIGNATIONS RUNNING IN PARALLEL]**
**[SYSTEM UNABLE TO RECONCILE]**
**[SYSTEM UNABLE TO RECONCILE]**
**[SYSTEM UNABLE TO RECONCILE]**

The words repeat like a litany. The screen freezes. The cosmic light surrounding Issei flares, bright enough that even the Watchers must look away.

For the first time in eternity, the classification matrix returns no answer.

### Reactions to Pull 1

The three judges of the Living Tribunal exchange glances. *Glances*. This is a gesture of uncertainty, and in the presence of the court, uncertainty is a confession of fallibility.

One of the judges—the aspect that is Law—turns to the assembled Watchers and speaks in a voice like the cracking of continental plates:

"The classification matrix has failed. This is... unusual."

The Watchers stir in their concentric circles. Their ancient eyes, which have watched the formation of galaxies and the death of gods, now focus entirely on the boy whose essence floats in the center of the chamber.

One voice rises from the assembly, ancient beyond measure:

"There is something here we do not comprehend."

Another Watcher echoes: "In all our observance, there are beings we do not fully understand. But we do not *fail* to comprehend. This is different."

The Living Tribunal's collective presence deepens. When it speaks, its voice carries the weight of every judgment it has ever made, every verdict it has ever rendered across the infinite multiverse.

"In all eternity, we have categorized every being who walks between worlds. Kings and beggars, gods and monsters, abstracts and mortals. Every being has a place in the order. Every being *belongs somewhere*."

A pause. A silence like the void between stars.

"This one... *exceeds our taxonomy*."

The cosmic chamber fills with unease. The streams of biographical data that flow through the walls seem to hesitate, as if even the fundamental records of existence are confused about how to file this information.

Loki, somewhere in the audience, leans forward with a grin. *"Secrets within secrets,"* he murmurs to Thor, who sits rigidly, his expression unreadable. *"The boy who cannot be judged."*

But there is no triumph in the observation. There is only the growing understanding that the court itself has encountered something beyond its authority.

## Gacha Pull 2: Censored Status

**Rarity:** Secret

The Living Tribunal is not one to accept failure. The judges confer silently—a communion that happens in the spaces between moments. They decide, collectively, to push deeper.

One final attempt. One more category.

The classification matrix initiates a *hidden protocol*.

It is not often used. This protocol reaches into the deepest files of the theatre's own consciousness, accessing classifications that are not meant for casual observation. These are the *true* designations, the ones reserved for beings whose existence touches the fabric of reality itself.

The matrix spins one final time.

And then—

**[DESIGNATION CLASSIFIED: ———— / ———— DEVOURER-CLASS RESISTANCE / THREAD TO ———— ARCHITECTURE]**

The screen *fractionalizes*.

The words appear on the cosmic display, brilliant and impossible, and then—

They *vanish*.

Not erased. Not forgotten. But *censored*. The classification system itself is being overridden by something higher. Something that exists *above even the Living Tribunal*.

The first blank space where a word should be is large enough to contain concepts that defy language. Whatever was written there, it has been redacted. Removed from sight.

The second blank space carries implications that make the assembled watchers lean back in horror.

*DEVOURER-CLASS RESISTANCE.*

What is a Devourer? What is a force so immense that resistance to it must be catalogued as a cosmic property? And why does Issei—human boy, devil, dragon, anomaly—why does *he* possess such resistance?

The third redaction is worst of all.

*THREAT TO ———— ARCHITECTURE.*

Threat. Not to a kingdom. Not to a world. Not to a universe.

*To Architecture itself.*

The fundamental structure of reality. The laws that bind the multiverse together. The cosmic code upon which all existence is written.

And someone, something, has decided that the nature of this threat must be *hidden*.

### Reactions to Pull 2

The cosmic chamber goes *silent*.

The Watchers do not speak. Even beings who have observed the creation and death of galaxies sometimes find silence to be the only adequate response.

The Living Tribunal's three aspects turn to face each other. This is not an exchange of glances anymore. This is a *confrontation*.

The aspect that is Judgment speaks first:

"The theatre has hidden something. This is unprecedented."

And it is true. The Living Tribunal is supreme within its own dominion. Nothing should be hidden from it. Nothing should override its access. Yet something has. Something has *censored the record itself*.

The aspect that is Law adds: "What role does this boy play that cannot be named? What classification is so significant that it must be obscured from us?"

The aspect that is Justice remains silent, but its presence grows heavier, as if contemplating implications that even the other judges are not yet ready to face.

The Watchers begin to understand.

One of them, whose memory stretches back to times before time, speaks with something approaching fear in its ancient voice:

"There is a power above even us. The theatre itself answers to something. It is being *controlled*. Its decisions are not its own."

Another Watcher adds: "The theatre is not neutral. We believed it was a neutral space, a place of observation that belonged to no faction, no ideology, no cosmic power. But we were wrong. The theatre *has masters*. And those masters have designated this boy as something important enough to censor from all of us."

Loki, in his section of the theatre, laughs. The sound is delighted and terrible.

"The theatre hides secrets. The theatre that is supposed to show us everything—it is lying by omission. This is delicious. This is *magnificent*."

But even Loki's triumph is undercut by unease. He too understands what the censorship means: there are powers greater than the Living Tribunal. Powers that can reach into the cosmic bureaucracy itself and *redact truth*.

The cosmic entities assembled in the chamber—the gods, the abstracts, the celestials—they begin to whisper among themselves. Unease. *Profound unease*.

They are being kept in the dark.

Issei Hyoudou, the human boy who bears infinite power and refuses to be categorized, is not merely a subject of observation. He is something more. He is *central* to the theatre's purpose. His true designation is hidden. And the theatre itself is subjugated to something greater.

That something cares deeply about Issei.

Or it cares deeply about protecting reality *from* Issei.

Or both.

The distinction, the cosmic chamber now understands, may be meaningless.

## Theatre Ledger

### Entry 1: Classification Failure

The Living Tribunal has encountered something beyond its taxonomy. In ten billion years of service, across infinite multiverses and countless beings of every conceivable type, the Tribunal has never failed to place a being in proper classification. Yet Issei Hyoudou remains *unfiledable*.

He is not mortal. He is not fully demon. He refuses godhood. He exceeds "anomaly" in scope and intentionality. Every subcategory breaks under the weight of his contradictions.

The very concept of *classification* itself has been challenged. If the supreme judges of the multiverse cannot categorize this one being, what does that say about the validity of categorization itself? What does it say about the order of the cosmos?

### Entry 2: The Censorship

For the first time in recorded history, the theatre's classification matrix has been *overridden*. A higher authority has taken the designation of Issei Hyoudou and hidden it from view. The blank spaces in the record are more terrifying than any designation could be.

DEVOURER-CLASS RESISTANCE.
THREAT TO ———— ARCHITECTURE.

What these mean remains a mystery. But the mystery itself is significant. The theatre does not keep secrets easily. The theatre is designed to *reveal*, to show, to expose all truths to those who watch.

Yet it hides this.

Someone, something, has mandated silence. And the theatre has *obeyed*.

### Entry 3: The Hierarchy Exposed

The cosmic entities have discovered a hierarchy they did not know existed. The Living Tribunal stands at the top of the known hierarchy—supreme, infallible, the final court of cosmic justice.

But above it, there is *something else*.

The theatre answers to masters unseen. Those masters have interests. Those masters have *agendas*. And those agendas involve a human boy from an Eastern high school who has inherited infinite power and refuses the throne.

The security of this hidden hierarchy has been breached. Knowledge now exists in the minds of watchers, judges, and gods. The secret that there is a secret has become public.

### Entry 4: The Question

What is Issei Hyoudou to the theatre? Why is he worth hiding from the universe itself?

Is he a threat that must be monitored?

Is he a shield that must be guarded?

Is he something that the architecture of reality itself *requires* to remain functional?

The Living Tribunal cannot answer. The Watchers cannot answer. Even the gods, in their vast knowledge, cannot answer.

Only the theatre knows.

And the theatre is not talking.
