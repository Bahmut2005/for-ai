# Chapter Fifty-Seven: Villains' Roundtable

**Primary Reaction Groups:** Victor Doom • Erik Lehnsherr • Wilson Fisk • Loki Laufeyson • Helmut Zemo • Nathaniel Essex • En Sabah Nur • Thanos

---

The villains' section of the theatre had always occupied an odd position — not quarantined, not exactly welcomed, but present. They sat on the far side of the room from the heroes' blocks, and the machine had been clear about the boundaries: no direct conflict during intermission, no leverage plays across the aisle, no deals struck in the dark while Marvel decided what Marvel would be.

It was a pragmatic arrangement. Villains, even villains from different universes with no common cause, understood pragmatism.

The theatre held them now, and the darkness of the screen seemed to suit them better than it had suited the Avengers. There was no anguish here, no moral arithmetic being worked out in real-time. There was only assessment.

Victor Von Doom had not moved from his seat in four hours. His armor was immaculate as always, but his hands were steepled in front of him, and his mind — everyone in the room could see it in the set of his shoulders — was *working*. Beside him, Erik Lehnsherr sat with the stillness of a man who had moved mountains and was weighing whether it was worth doing again. Wilson Fisk, massive and perfectly groomed, occupied a section of the seating that had been retrofitted to account for his size, and his eyes moved between the other villains like a merchant assessing inventory. Loki was cleaning his nails with a knife that had definitely not existed a moment ago, expression suggesting he was considering something amusing and possibly painful. Helmut Zemo, in formal dress despite or because of the theatre's informality, sat with the absolute confidence of old money and older grievances. Nathaniel Essex was taking notes — actual notes, on actual paper conjured from somewhere that suggested his resources were still intact inside this place. En Sabah Nur occupied the back of the section, literally the largest presence in the room, his eyes closed but his attention absolutely present. And Thanos — Thanos sat across a gap from everyone, as he did in most rooms, and his expression suggested that watching Marvel judge anything was the most profound entertainment available to the universe.

The screen chimed.

**GACHA PULL: 1 OF 2.**

**"VILLAINS DISCUSS ISSEI" — RARITY: RARE.**

The vision did not open gently.

It opened in a council chamber — not Latveria's, not Asgard's, not any real location — a space conjured by the machine's will, a conference table that stretched impossibly long, and the eight of them, as if they had always been meant to sit here together, as if the theatre itself had simply confirmed what had always been true.

Victor Doom spoke first, and his voice carried the weight of genuine consideration.

**"A craftsman of genuine skill."** He was looking at something none of the others could see — perhaps a blueprint, perhaps an architectural assessment, perhaps simply a measurement of a man against the standards Doom held only for those he considered equals. **"He has taken the base model — the dragon resonance, the amplification system, the knight/bishop/rook differentiation — and optimized it in ways that suggest no formal training. Self-taught mastery of a system as complex as his armor should not be possible. And yet."** He turned to face the table fully. **"He is misusing those talents. Dragon forms. Combat applications. Why does he not build empires? Latveria stands because I will it and have the power to make that will absolute. This boy has power to reshape nations, perhaps planets, and he uses it to *play soldier* in a school."**

Magneto's response was immediate, not argumentative but genuinely philosophical.

**"Because he has liberated thousands already, and he refuses to seize the permanent power to protect them."** Lehnsherr's voice carried the weight of a man who had made exactly the opposite choice. **"He raises mutants and humans alike to positions of strength within his system, but he does not consolidate that power into infrastructure that would outlive him. He is afraid of the monster he might become if he tried. Weakness, perhaps. Or the only wisdom anyone in this room has ever demonstrated."**

**"Irrelevant,"** Wilson Fisk said flatly, and pulled out a detailed file — impossible, impossible, but the theatre had learned long ago that impossibility was just a matter of will applied correctly. **"He is vulnerable through his cities. His parents remain in Japan, exposed to conventional leverage. His wives are scattered across realms, each one a pressure point. His allies are strong but not infinite. Cut one thread correctly, and the entire structure collapses. He is not incorruptible because he is invulnerable. He is only untouchable if we never find the right angle."**

Loki laughed — actually laughed, a sound like broken glass mixed with something sharper and more dangerous.

**"I have sent three recruitment offers."** The trickster set down his knife and examined his cuticles with theatrical disinterest. **"Three separate approaches, carefully calibrated for his psychology, each one containing genuine strategic value alongside the flattery. Each one returned with jokes. *Jokes*, and increasingly elaborate ones — he drew pictures on the last rejection. Issei Hyoudou mocks the God of Mischief, and worse, he makes it *hurt*, because the mockery is affectionate."** Loki's smile did not waver, but his voice dropped into something colder. **"I have corrupted kingdoms. I have turned brothers against each other. And this teenager plays with me like a cat with something beneath its attention."**

Nathaniel Essex looked up from his notes with the expression of a man who had just seen something that confirmed all his theories.

**"His genetics are exceptional."** Essex's voice carried the precision of someone describing a work of art he planned to dismantle for study. **"The dragon resonance is integrated at the chromosomal level — not a parasitic symbiosis, but a true fusion. If I could sequence the exact mechanism of the integration, understand how the amplification feedback loops without causing cellular breakdown... His genetics represent the next stage of human evolution. Why do you all debate his *allegiance*? His value lies in understanding his composition."**

**"Because he will not be dissected willingly,"** Thanos said, and his voice was patient in the way of someone who had seen a thousand worlds and found them all consistently predictable. **"He is a contradiction."** The titan turned his great head toward the table, and the council fell quiet in the way that things do when something larger than all of them speaks. **"He raises his power to planetary-level capacity, perhaps beyond. He accumulates strength, integrates systems, builds networks of allies and amplifiers. And then he refuses to use any of that strength for meaningful culling, for pragmatic reduction, for the difficult mathematics that balance requires. He claims to protect life, but he offers no sustainable framework. He hoards power without purpose. Ideologically incoherent."**

**"But powerful,"** Apocalypse said, and En Sabah Nur opened his vast eyes for the first time in the discussion. **"And the measure of strength is the only truth that survives." He leaned forward, and the table seemed to bend under the weight of his presence. **"Is he a mutant? His genetics show markers. The potency suggests evolutionary acceleration. If he could be brought into the fold as one of the evolved rather than the weak..."**

**"He cannot,"** Magneto cut in quietly. **"Because the first rule of his existence is choice. Mutants who join mutant causes join because they are told they must or die. This one — he would need to *want* to. And he wants nothing you are offering."**

Helmut Zemo, who had been silent until now, finally spoke with the precision of old Austrian nobility.

**"He is a symptom of the multiverse's cascading disorder,"** Zemo said. **"A single exemplar should never hold this much power unchecked. In a properly structured world, authority remains distributed, balanced by the natural forces of political counterweight. He is precisely what emerges when those structures fail — the superman, the inevitable power, the justification for every would-be tyrant to follow. He may refuse empire today. His heirs will not refuse tomorrow. He must be contained, not through force, but through political pressure, legal framework, the slow grinding machinery of civilization reminding him that individuals, however powerful, are not the solution."**

**"And if he refuses containment?"** Doom's voice was quiet again, but the question carried the weight of someone who had spent a lifetime refusing containment himself and respected the quality in others.

**"Then,"** said Thanos, **"the theatre will have rendered its judgment, and the infinite multiverse will have confirmed again that strength without wisdom creates only temporary dynasties. I will await his fall with the patience of one who has waited eons."**

The vision shimmered.

---

The council remained, but now it shifted — the scene moving backward, forward, into a dozen moments stacked like cards, and the theatre watched as eight different villains, in eight different approaches, tried to recruit or corrupt or convince or leverage Issei Hyoudou into service.

**GACHA PULL: 2 OF 2.**

**"RECRUITMENT ATTEMPTS" — RARITY: RARE.**

---

**Victor Doom's Approach — Formal, Royal, Honest:**

**"Latveria could use a royal craftsman. One who understands that power without purpose is merely chaos with better armor. Come. Build with me. We will reshape a nation into something the multiverse has not yet imagined."**

Issei, in his human form, dressed in casual clothing, sitting in what appeared to be a dormitory room, looked up from his phone and smiled with genuine warmth.

**"Lord Doom, I've read what you've built. The infrastructure design in Latveria is legitimately impressive — the geothermal integration, the distributed power grid, the way you've managed to advance technology without destabilizing the agrarian sectors. That's not just power. That's *craft*."** He paused. **"But I'm not the person for that work. I'm seventeen. I make mistakes. And I'm not ready to be anyone's royal anything. I respect what you're trying to do, but I have to build my own empire, not inherit someone else's vision."**

Doom, in the vision, inclined his head slightly — not defeat, but acknowledgment. And in the theatre, Victor Von Doom watched this exchange without moving, but something in his posture shifted, almost imperceptibly.

**"Respectfully declined,"** Loki's whisper cut through the moment.

---

**Loki's Approach — Flattery, Mischief, Charm:**

**"Imagine what we could achieve together! The trickster and the dragon king! We could remake the Nine Realms in our image. Comedy and conquest. You and I, Issei — we could make the multiverse *fun*."**

Issei laughed — genuinely laughed, the kind of laughter that carried real affection.

**"You know what? You remind me of someone who'd betray me by Tuesday. Seriously. I trust you as far as I can throw you, which — and I mean this with genuine respect — is pretty far given the dragon strength, but the point stands."** He grinned. **"But I like you anyway. You're honest about what you are, and that counts for something."**

Loki's expression in the vision was something between amusement and genuine anger, the two intertwined so thoroughly that it was impossible to separate them.

**"He laughs at me,"** Loki whispered to the council in the actual vision, and the words carried a venom that suggested this was the part that genuinely hurt. **"He sees through every stratagem. And he remains *affectionate* about it. How do you corrupt someone who will not stop being kind to you while rejecting you?"**

---

**Wilson Fisk's Approach — Leverage, Threat, Calculation:**

**"Your parents are vulnerable in Japan. Your teachers are known entities. Your allies scattered across realms. Cut one thread, and the entire structure becomes fragile. Work with me. Make this easy, and they remain untouched."**

The vision showed Issei's eyes — the human eyes, brown and innocent-seeming — shift to gold. Not angry gold. Not the gold of rage. The gold of a dragon that had just processed exactly what was being threatened, and exactly what would happen if the threat continued.

Issei stood, and the room seemed to compress around him, the very space responding to the dragon-presence underneath the boy-shape.

**"Try."** One word. Flat. Absolute. **"Touch my family, and I will end you so completely that the machine itself will have to file a new category for how thoroughly I erase your existence. Not as a threat. As a promise."**

The gold eyes returned to brown, and Issei sat back down as if nothing had happened.

**"Other than that, no."**

In the vision, Fisk had not moved, but everyone watching could see the calculation in his eyes — the moment he recognized that this angle was not merely closed, but actively dangerous to pursue.

**"Leverage fails,"** Fisk said, and it was the most honest thing he had spoken all night. **"He does not negotiate from a position of fear."**

---

**Nathaniel Essex's Approach — Scientific Fascination, Honest Proposition:**

**"Your genetics are... remarkable. I have studied enhancement extensively. You represent a synthesis I have never achieved. I would like to examine you. Not harm you. Understand you. Sequence the integration. Your worth to science alone—"**

Issei cut him off, voice firm and absolutely final.

**"I'm not for sale, and neither is my family. Not for research, not for study, not for any combination of good intentions and genuine scientific curiosity. I make my own choices about my body, and the answer is no."**

**"Noted,"** Essex said, and returned to his notes. The pragmatism of the rejection did not hurt because it was reasonable.

---

**Apocalypse's Approach — Power Recognition, Challenge:**

**"You are strong. But are you evolved enough to stand with true power? Will you take the next step? Become what you could be instead of what you merely are?"**

Issei's response was quiet but penetrating.

**"Power means nothing without choice. You don't offer choice. You offer conformity dressed up as evolution. I'm strong, yes. But I'm strong enough that I don't need to be stronger in ways that cost me who I am."**

Apocalypse, in the vision, did something unexpected — he smiled.

**"You may survive longer than most,"** the ancient mutant said. **"But survival is not victory."**

**"Neither is yours,"** Issei replied.

---

**Magneto's Approach — Genuine Alliance, Mutual Respect:**

The scene shifted. This one was different — not a threat or flattery, but actual negotiation.

**"Join me. Liberate mutantkind. Your power could change the equation entirely. We could end discrimination not through persuasion, but through the simple fact of demonstrated strength making oppression impossible."**

Issei considered this seriously, and the vision showed the weight of the decision moving through him.

**"I respect what you're trying to do,"** Issei said finally. **"But liberation enforced is just a different cage. It's a better cage, maybe. It's a cage built by someone who cares. But if I help you build it, then I'm building a system where people don't have the choice to refuse protection. They're safe, yes. But they're not free."**

**"Freedom will get your people killed,"** Magneto said, and it was not a threat but a fact.

**"Maybe,"** Issei replied. **"But it's their choice to make."**

Lehnsherr looked at the council, and the expression on his face was complicated — something between respect and profound disagreement, the kind of thing that could only happen when two people valued incompatible virtues equally.

---

The visions released them, and the council sat in silence for a long moment.

**"He cannot be corrupted through standard methodology,"** Thanos observed finally.

**"He will not be corrupted through any methodology,"** Magneto corrected. **"That is the specific nature of what he is. A young man, already, who has integrated his power so completely with his ethics that you cannot separate them. He is not restraining himself. He is not choosing the difficult path. He is being himself, and his self happens to include the mathematics of care."**

**"Then he is either a fool or a genuine threat,"** Zemo said coldly. **"Either way, containment remains necessary."**

**"Containment of what?"** Doom asked quietly. **"A boy who refuses power? The theatre will be unable to find a legal framework to justify holding him. And if it tries, it will only prove that all the talk of judgment is merely the strong deciding what the strong can do with the weak."**

The vision shimmered out.

**Secrets Revealed:**
- Issei has received recruitment offers from multiple villainous entities and has established a pattern of response: respectful declining where respect is offered, extreme escalation only when family is threatened. His threat response is absolute (promised complete erasure to Fisk) and genuine (dragon-eyes activate, room compresses).
- The machine classified these attempts as "Rare" rather than Epic or Secret, suggesting they are routine aspects of Issei's ongoing existence rather than anomalous. This indicates he is actively courted by multiple factions and has developed consistent rejection protocols.
- Issei's ethics are not imposed constraint but integrated identity — confirmed across all recruitment attempts. He does not choose the difficult path; the difficult path is who he is. This makes him incorruptible not through strength of will but through fundamental character structure.
- Magneto's offer was genuinely offered and genuinely considered, the only villain approach that received serious philosophical engagement rather than immediate rejection. This suggests Issei respects Magneto's cause even while disagreeing with the method.

**Alliance / Opinion Shifts:**
- **Villainous Consensus:** No faction believes Issei can be turned to their side. Assessment is unanimous: he is either incorruptible (Magneto, Lehnsherr's conclusion) or too dangerous to approach further (Fisk). Loki's personal frustration suggests emotional investment in recruitment failure, indicating possible obsession.
- **Doom's Respect:** Victor Von Doom's incline of the head after Issei's philosophical refusal indicates genuine acknowledgment of character quality. Despite seeing Issei as misusing his talents, Doom appears to accept Issei's right to that choice. This marks a respect-based opinion shift.
- **Fisk's Calculation:** Kingpin's withdrawal after dragon-threat suggests reassessment of cost-benefit. He does not accept the rejection—he calculates that the cost of pursuing leverage is now higher than any benefit gained. Contingent rather than philosophic retreat.
- **Essex's Pivot:** Nathaniel Essex returns to notes after philosophical rejection, treating Issei as closed case. Professional detachment, but absence of renewed pursuit suggests he accepts the refusal as final.
- **Magneto's Complicated Position:** Lehnsherr shows signs of internal conflict — he believes Issei is wrong about enforcement and freedom, but respects the choice and the reasoning. This is a villain experiencing genuine ideological engagement rather than simple rejection.

**Unresolved Hooks:**
- **If villains cannot corrupt or recruit Issei, and heroes cannot formally ally with him, what is the theatre's actual function in rendering judgment?** Doom's closing question — asking what legal framework could possibly justify containing someone who refuses power — opens the structural problem: the theatre may be attempting to judge someone for whom judgment has no practical application.
- **Loki's obsession:** The trickster's genuine frustration suggests Issei may become the object of continued recruitment attempts from Loki specifically. The fact that Issei "laughs with affection" while rejecting may be feeding rather than discouraging the pursuit. This could escalate beyond the current pattern.
- **Fisk's leverage strategy remains dormant.** The kingpin has not abandoned his plan — he has only determined that direct approach is dangerous. He may be shifting to indirect leverage, working through intermediaries, or planning longer-term pressure that does not require direct family contact. His withdrawal is tactical, not philosophical.
