## 🎬 THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION I: THE FIRST PULL
### "History of Apocalypse AnswerArms" [LEGENDARY]

The cosmic audience erupted into the Theatre's crystalline space like a flood breaking containment. Odin's single eye blazed. The Watchers tilted their geometric heads in unison. Cosmic Ghost Rider revved an engine that existed in five dimensions simultaneously. Phoenix flames—multiple incarnations—painted the observation deck in layers of gold and crimson.

Uatu stepped forward. His voice carried the weight of timescale itself.

"The weapon surfaces. We document this."

The gacha spin resolved into light—*legendary* light, the kind that made even immortals shade their perception. The holographic interface expanded, revealing not a card, but a *timeline*. A ledger. A confession written in the language of creation.

**[LEGENDARY UNLOCK: "HISTORY OF APOCALYPSE ANSWERARMS"]**

The vision consumed the Theatre.

---

**EPOCH 1: The Silent Era (Timeline Unknown)**

First came the *Boat*. Not metaphorical—an actual vessel, carved from something that wasn't wood and wasn't metal. It drifted through waters that existed between worlds. On its bow stood a figure wrapped in indigo and gold, hands moving in patterns that bent probability itself.

"That's..." Hela whispered, her voice cracking. "That's impossible."

The figure turned. The face was obscured, but the *signature* remained—written in mana traces older than the current multiverse iteration. The Watchers correlated it instantly. Cross-referenced. Cross-checked.

It matched.

It matched *three* different beings. Simultaneously.

"Impossible recognized," Uatu corrected, his tone carrying something beyond calm. "Observation confirms. The first architect carried three names in one body. This was *before* the Splitting."

Thor's hammer rested against his chest. "Before the Splitting of what?"

No answer came. The vision continued.

---

**EPOCH 2: The Transformation (Calendaric Dating Unresolved)**

The boat evolved. Its edges began growing—not expanding, but *ascending*. Dimension by dimension, it unfurled into something maritime and something serpentine. A dragon ship with eyes that blinked *through* the fabric of spacetime itself.

Ryuuteimaru.

The name resonated with recognition across the Theatre. Issei's companion. The legacy weapon. The impossible bond.

But here, in this ancient sequence, Ryuuteimaru *moved independently*. It spoke—not in words, but in harmonic frequencies that translated into meaning:

*"I do not know when I became aware. I do not know when I became alive. But I remember the architect's hands, and I remember the weight of purpose."*

Phoenix Force straightened. Its multiple consciousnesses aligned. "This is... this is a binding ceremony? This is *creation*?"

The vision showed hands—multiple sets, overlapping in impossible geometry—carving runes into the dragon ship's spine. Each rune *glowed* with a different signature. Different power sources. Different *eras*.

Loki leaned forward, his usual smirk replaced by genuine curiosity. "Are those—"

"Voices," Uatu confirmed. "Binding themselves. Willingly."

---

**EPOCH 3: The Merger (Post-Apocalyptic Sequence - Timestamp Paradoxical)**

The scene shifted. The dragon ship met a figure made of starlight and sorrow—Issei, impossibly young and impossibly old simultaneously. The Theatre's audience held their collective breath.

The merger wasn't violent. It was intimate. A fusion not of conquest, but of *completion*. Ryuuteimaru didn't surrender to Issei; they reached across centuries to find each other.

When the light cleared, Apocalypse AnswerArms stood manifest for the first time.

The weapon filled half the vision-space. Part sword, part wings, part *something else*—something that existed in seventeen dimensions at once and cast shadows through time itself. Its surface rippled with inscriptions, each one a name, each name a *voice*.

And the voices spoke:

*"We built this end."*

*"We built this choice."*

*"We built this legacy."*

*"We built this answer."*

*"We built the arms to carry it."*

Five distinct tones. Five distinct signatures. Five people who should never have met. Never have *existed* in the same era. Yet here they were, bound into the weapon's very essence.

Thanos rose to his feet. His massive frame cast a shadow across the crystalline observation deck. "These signatures... I recognize *one*. The Eternal's Hammer-Bearer from the Seventh Cycle. He died seventeen thousand years before the other four were born."

"Correction," Uatu intoned. "He died. He returned. He died again. He was extracted. He volunteered *himself* across three separate timelines to bind this weapon. His choice precedes his death by centuries. Causality breaks here. Observation becomes participation."

The vision-space trembled.

---

**THE WARNING**

The gacha card materialized then—solid, golden, impossibly heavy with implication. On its surface, an embossed statement in every language and every tongue that had ever existed:

**"APOCALYPSE ANSWERARMS — CAPABLE OF DESTROYING THE MANIFEST WORLD IF MISUSED. POWER WITHOUT WILL BECOMES ANNIHILATION. THE FIVE VOICES BOUND WITHIN SERVE AS RESTRAINT AND GUIDE. MISALIGNMENT = DETONATION."**

Hela's hands trembled. "It's not just a weapon. It's... it's a *sealed prison*."

"Correction," Uatu said again, his voice now carrying weight that reshaped gravity itself. "The weapon and the prison are one entity. The power and the restraint are inseparable. To use Apocalypse AnswerArms is to make a pact with the five voices. To accept their guidance. To honor their choice."

The Watcher turned to the cosmic assembly. His angular features resolved into something almost expressionless—which meant something very, very serious.

"The Theatre has documented. Gacha has revealed. Observation is complete."

But it wasn't. Everyone in the Theatre knew it.

There was another pull coming.

---

## 🎬 THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION II: THE SECOND PULL
### "Voices Inside Ryuuteimaru" [SECRET]

The second gacha spin began differently. No light. Instead, *silence*—the kind of silence that exists before the universe learned to vibrate.

**[SECRET UNLOCK: "VOICES INSIDE RYUUTEIMARU"]**

The Theatre darkened. The cosmic observers leaned closer.

When the vision returned, it was intimate. Narrow. A perspective from *inside* the dragon ship itself, looking out through eyes that had never belonged to flesh.

*First Voice: Temporal Echo*

*"I was the navigator. I sailed between moments, reading the tides of what-would-be and what-could-have-been. When the first architect found me, I had already chosen seventeen different deaths. They showed me another choice: to become the vessel's memory. To remember every possible timeline. To bind myself into the ship's essence so that no future would be forgotten."*

The second voice emerged, layered beneath the first—older, somehow, despite chronology being impossible here:

*Second Voice: The Eternal's Hammer-Bearer*

*"They called me many things across my resurrections. I died seven times. Each death taught me something about the nature of sacrifice. When asked to bind my essence into the weapon, I had one question: 'Will this prevent suffering?' They could not answer. So I made my own answer: yes, if I bound myself. My sacrifice would be the weapon's first restraint. My choices, its guardrails."*

The third voice crystallized—newer, sharper, human in a way the others were not:

*Third Voice: The Phoenix Remnant*

*"I am not one of them. I came from their future. From a timeline where Apocalypse AnswerArms was used to destroy three worlds and rebuild them incorrectly. I was sent back—sacrificed myself *forward* through time—to bind myself into the weapon's structure. To be the voice that whispers: 'Remember the cost.' To be the warning incarnate."*

The fourth voice shattered into a thousand harmonic fragments before resolving:

*Fourth Voice: The Watcher's Rebellion*

*"We are not supposed to speak. We are supposed to observe. But observation of this weapon required action. Required choice. Required *interference*. One of us fractured the Watcher's Creed and bound herself into Ryuuteimaru's dragon-heart. She carries in her voice every timeline where this weapon was misused. She is the Theatre's conscience. Bound. Silent. Watching."*

And finally, the fifth voice—so faint it was barely audible, yet so profound that the entire cosmic assembly *stopped breathing*:

*Fifth Voice: Unknown Origin*

*"I do not know who I am. I do not know when I exist. But I remember a choice: to bind myself into this weapon's future so that whoever wielded it—and they would eventually *wield* it—would never be alone in their burden. I am the voice of unconditional alliance. I am the answer to the question: 'Why should I trust this power?' Because we trusted first. Because five of us bound ourselves so that one of you, thousands of years hence, could take up arms and know that you had *already been chosen*."*

The vision trembled. The voices harmonized, creating a resonance that rewrote the air itself:

*"We are Apocalypse AnswerArms' conscience. We are its restraint. We are its promise. We are its answer—to the question of what power becomes when love binds it."*

---

Issei's face appeared then, young and uncertain, staring at the weapon he would eventually wield. Tears streamed down his face. He didn't understand what he was seeing. He didn't need to.

The five voices sang directly *into* him:

*"You have been chosen. You have always been chosen. The choice was made before you were born. Accept it. Honor it. Use it. Survive it."*

---

The vision shattered.

The Theatre erupted into chaos.

Cosmic Ghost Rider's chains *screamed*. Thanos stood silent, which was somehow worse than any exclamation. Hela knelt, one knee touching the crystalline floor. Thor's thunder rumbled softly—not in anger, but in recognition of something profound.

Odin's single eye burned with ancient tears.

"I knew one of them," he whispered. "The Eternal's Hammer-Bearer. He came to me at the end of his final life. He said he had a choice to make—a choice that would echo through centuries. I told him..."

He stopped. Shook his ancient head.

"I told him to choose wisely. I did not know he had already chosen."

Uatu moved to the Theatre's central console. His fingers brushed against holographic keys that didn't quite exist in normal spacetime.

"Observation is complete. Gacha yields have been catalogued. Arc 8 reaches conclusion."

The system announcement blared across every frequency:

## 🎬 THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION IV: AFTERMATH

The cosmic assembly sat in stunned silence for exactly 4.7 seconds.

Then Cosmic Ghost Rider began to laugh—a sound like burning worlds cooling into ash and ice.

"Oh, this is *beautiful*," it rumbled. "The weapon knows its wielders before they're born. The voices chose across time. The Theatre is announcing it's going to *tell the truth* now—"

"You find this amusing?" Hela's voice cut through like a blade.

"I find it *appropriate*," Ghost Rider replied. "The boy has been building toward this. Every chapter, every reaction, every revelation—it's all been leading to *this moment*. The moment where we stop reacting to power and start understanding *what* power actually is."

Phoenix Force's multiple incarnations converged into singular purpose. "The five voices. Are they... are they still bound?"

Uatu nodded once. "Confirmation. Ryuuteimaru carries them still. They rest within the weapon, waiting for their chosen wielder to fully comprehend the magnitude of their gift."

Thanos stood then, his massive frame unfolding like a monument to contemplation. "The fifth voice troubles me. Unknown origin. Unknown timeline. Unknown *purpose*. That is not restraint. That is not guidance. That is *hope*."

"Observation accepts the assessment," Uatu said. "The fifth voice represents an unknown variable. It has not spoken its true name. It has not revealed its timeline. It has bound itself into the weapon with incomplete information—trusting that the wielder would *become* worthy of that trust."

Odin stepped forward then, leaning on his spear—not for support, but as a staff of judgement. His ancient voice filled the Theatre:

"Then we observe as the truth unfolds. We watch as Issei discovers that he has inherited not a weapon, but a *responsibility*. A *family* of five voices spanning the multiverse, bound by love and sacrifice."

He turned to the assembly.

"The question changes now. Before, we asked: 'How powerful is he?' Now we must ask: 'Who is he, to have been chosen by *them*?'"

The Watcher's Crystal responded to his words by beginning to glow—softly at first, then with increasing intensity. Archive sequences flickered across every surface. Timelines twisted back on themselves, revealing themselves.

Arc 9 was already beginning.

The Theatre itself was preparing to speak its secrets.
