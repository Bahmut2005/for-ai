## Theatre Frame: The Crown's Judgment

The indestructible theatre's architecture **shifted**. The austere grey stone of the Avengers tribunal dissolved into something colder, more regal—emerald walls climbed toward impossible heights, adorned with the silver insignia of Latveria's coat of arms. A grand throne of iron and crystal dominated the space, and upon it sat **Victor Von Doom**, his crimson cloak draped like spilled wine, his metal mask catching the theatre's ethereal light in sharp, deliberate glints.

The crowd sensed the change in atmosphere. Doom never attended these tribunals lightly. His presence carried weight—the weight of a man who answered to no one, who had bent nations to his will, who spoke only when he had already decided the shape of the world.

Doom rose from his throne with the slow precision of a king certain of his authority.

"Issei Hyoudou," Doom began, his voice layered with metallic resonance, "has been measured by warriors, by tyrants, by the small minds of committees. Now he is measured by one who understands the *true* nature of power—not the crude application of strength, but the **mastery of creation itself**."

He descended the steps, each footfall echoing with finality.

"I have studied this boy. Watched him forge. Observed how his hands move with the certainty of one who shapes the very fabric of his purpose. He does not merely **make**—he **creates with intent**. And in that, I recognize kinship."

The theatre stilled. Mephisto's laugh rippled through the air—low, amused, dangerous.

Doom turned to address the full assembly, his gaze sweeping across the collected might of the Marvel multiverse with the indifference of someone surveying a chessboard.

"My judgment is thus: **Issei Hyoudou is neither ally nor threat to Latveria. He is something rarer—a fellow sovereign of the craft**. Should he ever wish it, the borders of my kingdom remain open. I would welcome him as I welcome few others: with **equality**, with respect for his mastery, and with the honour due to one who creates works worthy of eternity."

Reed Richards leaned forward, his expression a careful blend of curiosity and caution.

"That's not a judgment," Carol Danvers muttered. "That's a *recruitment pitch*."

"Why would Doom offer him equality?" Wanda asked, frowning. "Doom doesn't offer equality to anyone."

"Exactly," said T'Challa, his dark eyes narrowing. "Which means Doom sees something in Issei that he values more than dominance. That is... noteworthy."

Tony Stark's arms crossed. "Or it's the longest con ever recorded. Doom doesn't make friends. He makes *arrangements*."

## Gacha Pull I: Letter from Doom to Issei

The theatre's air **crystallized**. Golden light pooled at the stage's center, and when it cleared, a single object remained: a letter, written on parchment that shimmered with Latverian metalwork—silver filigree woven through the paper itself, indestructible and beautiful.

The words appeared in the theatre's collective consciousness, read aloud in Doom's own voice:

---

*To the Craftsman Hyoudou,*

*This theatre serves as intermediary where words between us might be witnessed. Know, then, that your reputation precedes you across dimensions. I do not flatter without purpose.*

*Your Boosted Gear is not merely magical. The craftsmanship—the **architecture** of its construction—speaks of a mind that understands the relationship between form and function. Between beauty and power. Between what **is** and what **should be**.*

*I have held the Infinity Stones. I have forged weapons that reshape reality. I have stood at the precipice of godhood. And I tell you without equivocation: the quality of your work would not be unworthy of Latveria's grand halls.*

*Should circumstances align, should you tire of the weakness that surrounds you in your current dimension, know that Latveria offers more than refuge. It offers **partnership**. A place where a craftsman of genuine mastery need not bow before lesser minds.*

*I am extending no summons. I am extending an invitation. Between craftsmen, this is the deeper respect.*

*There is also a gift enclosed with this correspondence. It is a work forged in the ancient Latverian tradition, in steel that has been tempered in the tears of dragons themselves. I would be... *interested* in your assessment of it.*

*With the regard due to a fellow master,*

*—Victor Von Doom, Sorcerer Supreme of Latveria*

---

The letter faded, but its implications hung in the air like smoke.

"He's trying to seduce Issei," Natasha said flatly.

"He's offering him sanctuary," corrected Thor, his voice thoughtful. "That is no small thing from Doom."

"He's showing off," Loki added with evident delight. "Doom wants Issei to know that *he* understands craftsmanship. That Doom himself is a master. Very transparent attempt to establish common ground through mutual superiority."

"But there's a gift," Valeria noted quietly, from where she sat in the observers' section. "That's the real message. Whatever Doom made, he's putting it forward for judgment."

Reed's eyes widened slightly. His daughter had just identified what he'd been processing—that Doom was, in essence, **challenging** Issei to critique his work. A dangerous move. Doom did not invite criticism lightly.

Tony watched the exchange between Reed and Valeria with dawning interest.

"Okay, so Doom's basically saying, 'You're good, I'm good, let's compare notes.' That's... almost respectful? If Doom were capable of respect, which he's not."

"This is recruitment," T'Challa murmured to his escort. "Doom believes Issei might choose Latveria over his current circumstances. He's offering safety and recognition. The question is: what does Doom want in return?"

Mephisto's laughter grew louder.

"Oh, this is delicious. Doom—**proud, magnificent Doom**—is so impressed by the boy's craftsmanship that he's willing to publicly acknowledge him as a peer. I wonder if Victor realizes how transparent his admiration truly is."

## Gacha Pull II: Valeria's Analysis of Dragon Tech

The second gacha pull manifested with the speed of a thought. This time, the theatre's vision fractured into two simultaneous perspectives: **Doom in his sanctum, and Valeria in her workshop**, separated by distance but connected by a single elegant creation between them.

The camera panned to reveal the gift Doom had sent: a blade. Not merely a blade—an *impossibility*. The metal shimmered with layers of forge-technique that seemed to contain entire star systems within its steel. Dragon-scales were worked into the fuller; runes glowed faintly along the edge. The craftsmanship was extraordinary.

**Valeria's hands moved across it with her father's analytical precision, but faster—always faster.** She ran her fingers across the metallurgical joints, examined the rune-work with a jeweler's loupe, tested the weight distribution with the certainty of someone who understood why each curve existed.

Her analysis took **seventeen minutes**.

In his private sanctum, Doom was still working. His hands, **bare and calloused**—a revelation in itself—shaped steel on a forge that burned with sorcerous fire. He was replicating the dragon-scales technique, layering temper and spell-work with meticulous care, his brow furrowed with concentration.

He was **trying to match it**. Trying to match *Issei's* level of craftsmanship.

The gacha revealed Doom's internal monologue, raw and unguarded:

*The boy layered the scales with precision that borders on the impossible. The spacing here—see how the overlap creates a secondary reinforcement that only becomes apparent under stress? I did not account for this the first time. Nor the second. Doom does not fail at craft, and yet...*

*The magic is different. The Japanese dragons—Ddraig—they work in the boy's favour. The draconic essence infuses every element. But the fundamental skill, the core understanding of metallurgy and design... that is not the dragon's gift. That is the boy's mind.*

*I will match this. I must understand how.*

Back in the workshop, Valeria set down her instruments and made a note on a data-pad:

*Father's work: Exceptional. Mastery of traditional Latverian forge-craft. Perfect execution of the design parameters he set for himself.*

*Issei's probable design: Advanced. The dragon-scale integration suggests active magical feedback during the forging process. He either intuited a technique that took Father fifteen years to learn, or the dragon is guiding his hands. Either outcome is... worthy of study.*

She paused, then added:

*Father made this gift to impress Issei. He's hoping Issei will be intimidated. Issei will not be. I wonder if Father knows that Issei will likely offer to teach him instead.*

In that moment, Valeria's assessment reached Doom's sanctum—as she knew it would, through the quantum-locked connection they shared. Doom **froze** mid-hammer-strike.

His daughter had taken his masterwork and, in less than twenty minutes, grasped not only its technical merit but also its **intent**. She'd seen through him. She'd understood that this gift was, at its core, an act of professional jealousy wrapped in courtly politeness.

For a moment—**just a moment**—Doom's shoulders tensed with a emotion that was not quite anger, not quite shame, but something between: the acute discomfort of being seen too clearly by someone he respected.

Then he set down his hammer and returned to his work with renewed focus.

The theatre watched in silence as Doom forged into the night, each strike of his hammer against steel driven by something more complex than mere pride: **the desire to understand, to match, perhaps even to exceed the craftsmanship of a boy who wielded a dragon as his partner**.

## Reactions: The Conspiracy of Craftsmanship

"Doom is *jealous* of Issei's work," Carol said, her voice carrying disbelief.

"Not jealous," corrected Shuri, who had been silent until now. "Inspired. Doom recognizes a rival craftsman, and he wants to understand how Issei achieved what he achieved. That's different from jealousy. That's *hunger* for knowledge."

"It's also dangerous," Tony countered. "Because Doom doesn't just want to understand. Doom wants to *master* it. And when Doom masters something, Doom tends to use it to reshape his corner of reality."

Reed sat motionless, watching Valeria in the gacha's aftermath. His expression was a careful study in fatherly pride and scientific concern.

"Valeria's analysis was faster than Doom expected," he said quietly. "She understood the intent behind the gift faster than Doom himself fully articulated it. That's... that's going to make him uneasy."

Mephisto's laughter filled the theatre again, delighted and cruel.

"Oh, Victor is *delicious* when he's frustrated. The fact that his daughter has seen through his grand gesture of professional respect and identified it as professional *competition*—that will eat at him beautifully."

T'Challa made a note in the leather journal he kept throughout these proceedings.

"Doom has declared Issei an equal sovereign and a craftsman of note. He's sent him a masterwork as both gift and challenge. If Issei responds with grace, accepting the gift while offering to teach Doom something new, he will have solidified an alliance with the most brilliant mind in Latveria—and inadvertently made an enemy of Doom's pride."

"And if he doesn't respond?" asked Stephen Strange.

"Then Doom will become obsessed with proving his superiority," T'Challa said. "Either way, Latveria is no longer neutral in Issei's fate. Doom has invested his *reputation* in the acknowledgment of Issei's mastery. He can't take that back without losing face."

Wanda nodded slowly, understanding the deeper game. "So Doom's invitation isn't just about recruitment. It's about Doom needing to be *right* about Issei. If Issei turns out to be a threat, Doom has to be the one to stop him. If Issei turns out to be magnificent, Doom needs to be recognized as the one who *understood* his magnificence first."

"Exactly," T'Challa confirmed. "We are watching the early stages of an alliance forged between two proud craftsmen, each one trying to prove to the other that they are the true master."

Reed leaned back, his mind already extrapolating the future. "This complicates the geopolitical calculus significantly. If Latveria becomes a sanctuary for Issei—if Doom succeeds in making him feel valued in a way no one else has—then we've essentially lost a potential ally and gained a probable enemy."

"Or," Valeria said from the observers' section, "we've identified someone who understands the value of what Issei represents. Doom respects power, yes, but he respects *creation* more. If Issei can create at the level Doom believes he can, then Doom's respect is absolute."

"Children shouldn't be this perceptive," Natasha muttered.

## Hook: Wakanda Awaits

As the gacha images faded, the theatre's architecture began its next transformation. The emerald and silver of Latveria dissolved, replaced by the warm golds and deep purples of **Wakandan architecture**. Vibranium glinted from impossible angles. The sound of ancient drums echoed softly in the distance.

T'Challa stood, adjusting his robes with deliberate ceremony.

"Doom has made his position clear," he said, addressing the full assembly. "He has declared Issei a sovereign craftsman and extended the hospitality of Latveria. This changes the calculation for Wakanda's analysis."

"Which is coming next," Tony said, watching the stage shift. "Let me guess—Shuri's going to take apart one of Issei's creations and figure out something we've all missed?"

"Or," Reed said thoughtfully, "Wakanda is going to offer Issei something Doom cannot: a nation where craftsmanship serves the *people*, not the ambitions of a single ruler."

The theatre stilled, anticipating the next judgment.

In the distance, the sound of Wakandan ceremony grew louder.

## Theatre Ledger

### New Pulls
- **"Letter from Doom to Issei"** (Rare): Formal invitation offering Latverian sanctuary and professional equality; reveals Doom's genuine admiration for Issei's craftsmanship and implicit challenge in the form of an enclosed gift.
- **"Valeria's Analysis of Dragon Tech"** (Rare): Reveals Doom's private forging efforts to match Issei's work; exposes Doom's jealousy and hunger to understand dragon-influenced metallurgy; Valeria's rapid assessment embarrasses Doom by seeing through his true motivations.

### Secrets Revealed
- Doom is not merely offering political alliance—he is professionally invested in understanding and matching Issei's craftsmanship.
- Doom spends his private time forging in secret, attempting to replicate techniques he observes in Issei's work, driven by both rivalry and admiration.
- Valeria Richards can analyze complex metallurgy faster than Doom's own expectations, suggesting she will become a key figure in evaluating Issei's capabilities.
- The dragon-scale integration in Issei's work represents either intuitive genius or active magical guidance from Ddraig.

### Alliance / Opinion Shifts
- **Doom's Position**: Declared Issei an equal sovereign and honoured craftsman; extended sanctuary in Latveria with the weight of personal respect.
- **Latveria's Stance**: No longer neutral; implicitly aligned with recognizing Issei's value and potential threat if he chooses hostile action.
- **Mephisto's Interest**: Renewed amusement in watching Doom's professional jealousy and pride conflict.
- **Tony's Concern**: Growing wariness about Latveria's potential recruitment of Issei; sees Doom's strategy as a long-term power move.
- **T'Challa's Diplomatic Calculus**: Recognizes that Doom's public acknowledgment of Issei as a peer has created a geopolitical shift; anticipates Wakanda's need to offer something Latveria cannot.
- **Valeria's Influence**: Established as a perceptive analyst who sees through parental pride and recognizes the deeper game Doom is playing.

### Unresolved Hooks
- Will Issei accept or reject Doom's invitation? How will Issei respond to being offered equality by a ruler of nations?
- What does Doom truly want from Issei—alliance, knowledge, apprenticeship, or domination disguised as respect?
- Can Valeria's assessment of the dragon-forged weapon reveal something about Issei's potential that Doom has overlooked?
- How will Wakanda's judgment differ from Doom's, and what will Wakanda offer that Latveria cannot?
- Will Doom's private forging efforts lead to a breakthrough that changes his understanding of draconic metallurgy?
- Is Issei aware that he now has two of the smartest minds in their respective dimensions studying him with intent?
