# Story Bible — Marvel Reacts to Canon Issei Hyoudou

**Read this before writing any chapter.** It defines the house style, the rules,
and the running continuity so every chapter stays consistent. Also read:
- `canon-dossier.md` — Issei's canon powers, arsenal, relationships, timeline.
- `100-chapter-outline.md` — find YOUR chapter's entry (title, Primary Reaction
  Groups, Gacha Pulls, and summary of beats). Follow it.
- `chapter-01.md` — the reference for house style/voice. Match it.

## Premise (never re-explain at length; the audience already lives it)

The entire Marvel multiverse — Avengers, X-Men, Defenders, Midnight Suns, the
Ravagers/Guardians, the villains, the gods, and the cosmic tier — was pulled into
an indestructible cosmic **theatre** with no exits. A cold system-screen shows
them the life of **Issei Hyoudou** (protagonist of *High School DxD*), one
**gacha pull** at a time — each pull an immersive, unskippable vision of a memory,
power, weapon, ally, or secret from his life. They cannot affect the theatre or
leave. They can only watch, argue, and change their minds. The theatre is
secretly preparing them, across 100 chapters, for a multiversal threat.

## House style (match chapter-01.md exactly)

- Open with the H1 title: `# Chapter N: <Title>` (spell the number as a word, e.g.
  "Chapter Sixteen").
- Then two HUD-style lines, blank line between them:
  `**Primary Reaction Groups:**` (the factions in focus, bullet-separated with •)
  and `**Gacha Pulls:**` as a numbered list, each `1. "Name" — **Rarity**`
  (names + rarities ONLY, no descriptions).
- Then `---`, then the narrative.
- Third-person, polished, novelistic prose. Target **~2,500 words per chapter**
  (a tight, focused chapter — hit the beats well rather than padding).
- Structure each chapter as: brief theatre framing → the gacha pull(s) playing as
  vivid immersive vision(s) → faction reactions, arguments, and ideological
  clashes → a hook into what's next.
- Use `---` scene dividers. Use `**[BOOST.]**`-style bold brackets for Boosted
  Gear's system voice and **bold** for Ddraig's dragon-voice dialogue.
- End EVERY chapter with `## Theatre Ledger` containing four bold-bulleted
  subsections: **New Pulls This Chapter**, **Secrets Revealed**,
  **Alliance / Opinion Shifts**, **Unresolved Hooks**.
- Vary the lens by faction, not the facts: street-level heroes react to the human
  cost; Avengers-class to power jumps; antiheroes to his kill-restraint and moral
  lines; gods/cosmic to god-tier feats; villains to the fact that he's immature
  AND incorruptible, which makes him impossible to co-opt.

## Content rating (hard rule)

Teen-rated. Issei is canonically a comedic pervert who dreams of being "Harem
King," and his world is full of romantic entanglements — keep all of that at the
level of embarrassing sitcom gags and innuendo. **Never** write explicit sexual
content, nudity description, or anything past a PG-13 comedic register. Techniques
like "Dress Break" are played as slapstick — cut away to reactions, never
describe bodies. The murders, wars, and grief are played straight and can be
visceral in emotional weight, but avoid gratuitous gore.

## Continuity — running state (as of end of Arc 1 / chapter 10)

**Core facts the room has learned about Issei:**
- Murdered by the fallen angel **Raynare** on a fake first date (light spear
  through the chest). Frank Castle and Wolverine have privately "marked" her.
- Resurrected as a **Devil** by **Rias Gremory** using ALL EIGHT of her Pawn
  Evil-Pieces — an abnormal, wildly expensive expenditure. Mephisto is convinced
  this proves Issei is secretly priceless; the room wants to know what Rias saw.
- Wields **Boosted Gear** (Longinus-class Sacred Gear) housing **Ddraig**, the
  ancient **Welsh Dragon Emperor**. "Boost" doubles his power every 10 seconds
  (uncapped in theory, limited by his body); "Transfer" redistributes that power
  to allies/objects; "Penetrate" bypasses defenses. Ddraig calls him "partner"
  and is developing genuine fatherly warmth toward him.
- Unlocked **Scale Mail** (Balance Breaker armor); mountain-erasing output.
  Teased: **Illegal Move Triaina** (Knight/Rook/Bishop specialized forms, incl. a
  battlefield-leveling "Dragon Blaster"). Arc 1 ended on "TUTORIAL COMPLETE."
- Loves and fiercely protects the persecuted healer **Asia Argento**. Swore a
  reckless, dragon-backed **oath against Heaven** over her mistreatment, which
  raised the theatre's ominous "threat/potential" meter.
- Earns respect through dogged failure, not raw talent — most notably a respected
  loss to **Sairaorg Bael** (a devil built purely on hard work, zero powers).

**Standing character/faction attitudes to carry forward:**
- **Doom & Doctor Strange:** enemies, but hold a working truce of scientific/
  mystical curiosity over Issei's unfamiliar cosmology.
- **Thor:** deep old-story reverence for Ddraig as an elder dragon. **Loki:**
  covetous of the bound dragon, always angling. **Fin Fang Foom:** scornful that
  a "real" dragon serves a human.
- **Captain America, Cyclops, Shang-Chi:** respect his grit/coachability.
- **Mephisto:** delighted, treats Rias's 8-Pawn spend as insider proof of value.
- **Street-level heroes (Spider-family, Defenders):** protective; grieved his
  murder.
- **The Watcher (Uatu):** structurally unsettled — Issei exists in NO timeline he
  has on record, yet he was bound here to learn him.
- **Cosmic tier (Silver Surfer, etc.):** unmoved by a single death, but watching
  closely.
- **The recurring prophecy line the screen flashed:** "HE IS THE ONE WHO WILL
  STAND BEFORE THE THINGS YOU CANNOT." (Seed/callback this across the arcs; it
  pays off in Arc 10 with the Unwritten Devourer, a cosmic enemy made of erased
  timelines and discarded futures.)

**Canon guardrails (don't contradict; see canon-dossier.md for detail):**
- His eight canonical fiancées/brides, in engagement order: Rias, Akeno, Xenovia,
  Irina, Asia, Koneko, Kuroka, Rossweisse. Others love him but aren't yet formal
  brides: Ravel, Kunou, Le Fay, Elmenhilde, Ingvild, Yasaka. Present-day
  biological children are UNSPECIFIED in canon (named kids like Airi Hyoudou are
  from a possible future ~30 years out, *High School DxD EX*) — Arc 10 uses an
  "erased future child" as a plot device; don't contradict this by treating
  present kids as canon.
- Power escalation order (do not skip ahead / spoil later forms in early chapters):
  Boosted Gear → Scale Mail → Illegal Move Triaina → Cardinal Crimson Promotion →
  Juggernaut Drive → Diabolos Dragon God → Pseudo Dragon Deification → True Dragon
  Deification → Apocalypse AnswerArms.
- He rarely kills humans, forgives former enemies, refuses to exploit vulnerable
  women, and carries lasting fear of romantic rejection from Raynare's betrayal.

When your chapter reveals something new, it becomes continuity for later chapters —
so keep your Theatre Ledger accurate; it is the memory the next writer relies on.
