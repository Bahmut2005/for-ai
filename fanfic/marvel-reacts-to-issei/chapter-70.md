## THE TRANSFORMATION

The stage erupted into a pocket dimension, a void of cosmic scale. Tartarus emerged first—not a being, but a **prison**. An ancient, multidimensional lockbox that had contained apocalypses, horrors beyond imagining, the screams of civilizations erased from time itself. It manifested as something between a creature and a fortress: chains of infinite length, walls of crystallized suffering, a core that pulsed with the weight of imprisoned eternities.

Then Issei appeared.

But he did not stand alone.

At his side was Ryuuteimaru—transformed beyond recognition. The familiar, who had begun as a small white boat, had evolved through every arc: dragon ship, armored companion, and now **fusion partner**. Ryuuteimaru was no longer separate. It was becoming. The scales of a dragon wrapped around Issei's frame, but not like armor. They were part of him. The ship-form's geometric plating merged with biological perfection. The dragon's wings spread—not just two, but a constellation of them, each radiating with the direct aura of **Great Red**, the ultimate dragon entity.

Issei himself was no longer humanoid.

He was transformation incarnate: a being of exoskeleton and cosmic radiance, half-dragon, half-machine, half-myth. His form defied singular description. The armor was alive. The wings hummed with reality-warping frequencies. His eyes—there were dozens of them, each seeing across different dimensional spectra—locked onto Tartarus with unified purpose.

*This* was **Apocalypse AnswerArms**.

The theatre held its breath.

Issei raised his primary arm. A blade of pure apocalyptic light materialized—an Apocalypse Ascalon. But then another. And another. Not one legendary weapon, but an arsenal. Ascalon blades materialized across his frame like stars in a hostile constellation. Each one bore the weight of a god-killer. Each one sang with the frequency of extinction.

Behind him, the **Revelation Barrier** unfurled—not a simple shield, but a dimensional bulwark that warped reality itself. It didn't just defend against attacks; it rewrote the space it occupied, inverting causality, turning offense into vulnerability, making any attempt to pierce it fundamentally paradoxical.

And across Issei's frame, all-range blasters aligned: cannons, beam-emitters, energy vortex generators, each one capable of firing at planetary scales.

The battle began.

Tartarus attacked—chains lashing out with the force of collapsing star systems. It was ancient. It was vast. It had held the universe's nightmares.

It was completely overwhelmed.

Issei did not dodge. He did not struggle. He simply *was*—and where he *was*, Tartarus could not be. The Revelation Barrier encountered Tartarus's first volley and did not break; it inverted. The prison found itself imprisoning its own attacks, collapsing inward. Issei's Ascalon blades moved not with the speed of light, but with something faster—the speed of inevitability. They shattered Tartarus's structure as easily as one breaks glass.

The all-range blasters fired.

The void itself screamed.

Tartarus, which had contained multitudes, which had endured eons, was simply erased. Not destroyed. Erased. As if it had never existed. Its chains fell to cosmic dust. Its core scattered across dimensions like scattered prayers. The ancient prison, the repository of eternal suffering, was gone in seventeen seconds.

Issei hovered in the void, still transformed, still terrible. Around him, the residual energy of his assault painted constellations of apocalypse across the darkness.

Then he powered down.

The transformation faded, but not instantly. Ryuuteimaru separated slowly, gently, as if both dragon and boy were exhausted from the effort of being unified. Issei collapsed to one knee, breathing heavily, his skin pale, his eyes dimmed. Even in victory, he was wrung out—the form's cost immediately visible in how he trembled.

The stage went dark.

## THEATRE LEDGER — SESSION 70

### **Entry One: The Form Achieved**
Apocalypse AnswerArms represents not merely an evolution of power, but a fundamental shift in category. Issei is no longer a god-tier individual. He is now classified as a cosmic-scale entity, capable of planetary destruction in a single strike. The transformation is the culmination of all previous forms: his divine heritage, his dragon power, his bond with Ryuuteimaru, his mastery of the Ascalon. But this culmination carries a price that makes repeated use impossible. He cannot rely on this form. Therefore, his future battles cannot assume its availability. Threat assessment updated across all factions.

### **Entry Two: The Cosmic Reckoning**
Galactus has classified this development as a Planetary-to-Cosmic Emergency, elevating Issei's threat potential to the highest tiers of multiversal concern. The Living Tribunal has noted that standard measurements fail to categorize his power. Odin has recognized in Issei a kindred attribute: knowledge of one's own power, and the terrible choices that accompany such knowledge. Earth's leaders have reoriented their strategic thinking around a single operational fact: Issei must remain an ally. The cost of his enmity is incalculable.

### **Entry Three: The Cost Made Visible**
The great revelation of this session is not that Issei can destroy worlds. It is that doing so nearly destroys him. The Apocalypse AnswerArms form lasts only sixty to ninety seconds at full power before user exhaustion becomes life-threatening. Recovery requires seven to ten days. Overuse risks permanent form dissolution and death. This limitation is not weakness. It is tragedy. It means that the most powerful weapon Issei possesses is also the weapon he can least afford to use. His greatest strength is also his greatest vulnerability.

### **Entry Four: The Transition**
Arc Seven concludes with a statement: we have seen what he can do. Arc Eight begins with a question: what will he do now? The Theatre has collected seventy chapters of power escalation. It has documented every form, every transformation, every increase in capability. But now, halfway through the story, the inquiry shifts. Power is answered. Truth remains. The remaining chapters will reveal not his abilities, but his character. Not his capacity for destruction, but his reasons for restraint. Not what he is capable of, but what he chooses to be.
