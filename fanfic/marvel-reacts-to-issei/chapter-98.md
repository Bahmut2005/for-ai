## THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION I: THE FIRST IMPACT

**[HUD Status: Coalition Defense Online | Reality Stability: COMPROMISED | Erasure Events: MULTIPLYING]**

The Devourer arrived not with fanfare but with *absence*.

It appeared as a void in space-time, a hole in the concept of existence itself. Reality didn't warp around it—reality *withdrew* from it, leaving an expanding bubble of unmade possibility in its wake. Everything touched by the Devourer simply ceased to have ever been. Cities erased from history. People unwritten from time. Futures pruned back to their roots and left to wither.

Forty-seven timelines died in the first thirty seconds.

"It's moving through the probability tree," Xavier's voice came through the psychic network, his tone shifting from calm to grimly urgent. "Every timeline it touches, it's eliminating. Not fighting them. Erasing them."

"Defenses?" Steve Rogers's mental voice cut across the network.

"Holding," Strange reported. "Magical binding circle is containing the expansion, but every second it holds costs us power. The Devourer doesn't attack the circle. It *consumes* it. Like we're trying to stop a fire with gasoline."

Then Issei moved.

The dragon-man didn't transform immediately into Apocalypse AnswerArms. He ascended through forms. First: Pseudo-Sekiryuutei, his balanced state, the form where human consciousness remained dominantly present. His hands glowed with red-and-white power as he positioned himself at the circle's edge.

"I'm engaging," he announced.

"You'll be erased!" Rocket shouted across the network.

"Not if I keep moving," Issei said.

He leapt forward, directly into the Devourer's influence—and immediately the sensation hit him: the feeling of being *unmade*. Every cell in his body screaming as the Devourer's touch tried to erase him from existence retroactively. But Issei's nature as a dragon-man, as someone who had been rewritten, rebuilt, and reconstructed across multiple timelines, gave him resistance. He couldn't exist *outside* erasure, but he could exist *despite* it.

He threw an energy blast directly into the Devourer's mass.

It passed through harmlessly. The Devourer didn't have a body to damage. It was the absence of body, the void where existence should be.

"True Dragon Deification," Issei said, letting his form cascade upward. His power multiplied. His wings manifested—not just the two physical ones, but twelve more forming in dimensional space. His eyes opened across his body. Scales emerged, hardened, arranged themselves in patterns designed for cosmic warfare. "I'm going higher."

"Don't," Jean's voice carried warning. "Our projections show that every form transformation costs you potential recovery time. You're burning through your reserves."

"I know," Issei said.

He reached Pseudo-Diabolos Dragon: the form where his draconic heritage achieved fullest expression while consciousness remained. Massive, terrible, a being of such overwhelming power that stars bent light around him. But even Diabolos Dragon, facing the Devourer, found itself ultimately ineffective. The form's power was *destructive*. The Devourer was not destruction. It was *negation*.

"He'll die before he reaches Apocalypse AnswerArms," T'Challa said quietly.

"Yes," Doom agreed. "But he will do so buying time for the coalition to adapt."

Issei's roar shook multiple dimensional barriers. "Then I go Apocalypse!"

**GACHA PULL 1 OF 2: "BATTLE AGAINST UNWRITTEN DEVOURER" [APOCALYPSE RARITY]**

The transformation was catastrophic.

Issei didn't just ascend to Apocalypse AnswerArms—he *forced* the form into manifestation while still bearing the burden of Diabolos Dragon. For an instant, he existed in two states simultaneously: full draconic fury and full apocalyptic weaponization unified into a singular being.

Ryuuteimaru manifested not as a separate entity but as integrated armor. The ship-dragon form wrapped around Issei's draconic body like a living weapon. Scales merged with geometrically perfect plating. Wings became infinite. And across his frame, weapons materialized: Ascalon blades, all-range blasters, dimensional cannons, reality-warping barriers, everything Apocalypse AnswerArms was capable of generating.

He was a god-destroying engine given form.

The battle began in earnest.

Issei's Ascalon blades sliced not at the Devourer, but at the *erasure*. He understood instinctively what Strange had understood: you cannot attack what isn't there. So instead, he attacked the *space* where the Devourer was unmade, carved through the void it created, forced it to *exist* in ways that made it vulnerable.

The Revelation Barrier erupted across multiple dimensional boundaries. Instead of simple defense, it inverted causality on a massive scale—wherever the Devourer tried to unmake a timeline, the barrier rewrote that unmake-event backward, forcing the Devourer to destroy what it had already destroyed. The logical paradox began weakening its fundamental nature.

"He's making it fight," Magneto observed, understanding the strategy. "Instead of trying to kill the Devourer, he's forcing it into patterns where its own negation becomes self-contradictory."

The all-range blasters fired—planetary-scale discharge across seventeen dimensions simultaneously.

The void screamed.

It was not a sound in any conventional sense, but something deeper—the sensation of unrealized potential crying out as it was forcibly brought into being, only to be immediately negated. Each blast from the blaster array hit the Devourer with enough force to reshape galactic structures. But the Devourer simply *absorbed* the damage into itself, grew larger with each strike, expanded its erasure radius to consume new timelines in response.

"He's not winning," Rocket said urgently. "He's just... slowing it down."

"Then we use the slowdown," Strange snapped. He threw his power directly into supporting Issei's barrier, feeding it magical energy from the binding circle. Loki joined him, adding sorcerous weight. Xavier and Jean pushed psychic reinforcement. All of Marvel's magic users combined their efforts, channeling power directly into Issei's defense.

Issei screamed.

He was experiencing apocalypse-level exhaustion while still maintaining the form. His cells were breaking down microsecond by microsecond. His dimensional alignment was fracturing. But he *held*. He held the barrier. He held against the Devourer's erasure. He held the line so that Marvel's reality wouldn't simply cease to have existed.

"The form duration is compromised!" Jean screamed across the network. "He's overloading! He's going to burn out!"

Then the wives arrived.

Rias and Akeno and Xenovia and Rossweisse all manifested directly in the combat space—manifested in the *realm of erasure* itself, where existing should have been impossible. They were no longer supporting from safety. They were fighting directly.

Rias threw her devil power into the barrier, amplifying its paradoxical strength. Akeno's lightning danced through the void, not trying to harm the Devourer but trying to *structure* it, give it shape and form that made negation impossible. Xenovia's holy magic carved through the Devourer's influence like a knife through shadow, creating small pockets of restored reality in its wake. Rossweisse sang binding wards with her full voice, a Valkyrie song of protection that resonated across every timeline simultaneously.

"We're here," Rias said, and her voice cut through even Issei's apocalyptic exhaustion. "You're not alone in this."

The Devourer, facing four additional sources of power *and* an army of cosmic allies positioned throughout the multiverse, began to *retreat*.

Not flee. Retreat. It was an intelligent entity, and it recognized that destroying Marvel's primary timeline had become tactically impossible. Instead, it began withdrawing into unrealized possibility—the spaces between timelines where nothing should exist.

Issei pursued.

In his Apocalypse AnswerArms form, burning through literally his entire remaining lifespan in exchange for this moment, he chased the Devourer into probability space. His power, amplified by his wives' love, by the coalition's support, by Marvel's accumulated magical strength, crashed down on the Devourer again and again.

The Devourer tried to unmake him.

The wives pulled him back into existence.

It was a cycle, repeated again and again, until finally—

**GACHA PULL 2 OF 2: "RETREAT INTO POSSIBILITIES" [SECRET RARITY]**

—the Devourer ceased fighting and instead *fractured*.

It didn't die. It didn't truly cease. But it *split* into components, each fragment scattering into different probability timelines, each one hiding in the spaces between moments where existence and non-existence blurred together.

The void collapsed.

Reality stabilized.

Issei fell.

## THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION II: THE AFTERMATH

**[HUD Status: Combat Sequence CONCLUDED | Reality Stability: RESTORED | Timeline Integrity: RECOVERING | Subject Status: CRITICAL]**

The coalition rushed to the combat site even as the dimensional barriers came down and Doom's sealed fortress reopened to the wider universe.

Issei lay in the center of an enormous crater, his Apocalypse AnswerArms form already collapsing back to human shape. His body was devastated—cells barely holding together, skin charred from dimensional friction, power reserves completely drained to zero and beyond.

But his eyes were open.

"Did we win?" he asked weakly.

"You held," Strange said, kneeling beside him. "You bought enough time for the coalition to adapt. The Devourer's still out there, still existing in probability space, but it's fragmented. It can't concentrate its power anymore. It can't create a coherent erasure event."

"So it'll just attack again," Issei said.

"Eventually," Doom confirmed. "But fragmented, it can be contained. Managed. Each fragment can be addressed independently. You've transformed an apocalyptic threat into a manageable one."

The wives were already surrounding him, hands moving across his damaged form, trying to will him to survive through sheer force of love.

Rias pressed her forehead to his. "You absolute idiot. You burned through decades of life. You nearly *ended* yourself."

"Had to," Issei managed a weak smile. "You needed me alive today. That's all that matters."

"Your lifespan matters," Akeno said, her voice sharp with barely controlled fury. "Your *future* matters."

"Will he recover?" Rocket asked practically.

"Yes," Rossweisse said, her magical diagnosis spreading across Xavier's network. "But it will take months. He's pushed beyond every safe threshold. His cells need time to re-synchronize. His dimensional alignment needs to stabilize. He won't be able to access any combat forms for at least three months. Possibly six."

"That's acceptable," Magneto said. "The Devourer is no longer an immediate threat. Three months gives us time to establish long-term containment protocols."

"Will it come back?" T'Challa asked.

"Eventually," Strange said. "But not as an active threat. More as a permanent feature of the multiverse's threat landscape. Something to be monitored. Contained. Managed as a chronic problem rather than an acute one."

## THEATRE LEDGER — SECTION III: THE COALITION HOLDS

**[HUD: Battle Assessment — INCONCLUSIVE BUT TACTICAL VICTORY | Coalition Status — MAINTAINED | Subject Recovery Protocol — INITIATED]**

In the days that followed, something unprecedented happened: the coalition *didn't disband*.

Doom offered Latveria's medical resources. Stark provided cutting-edge technology. Strange maintained magical life-support. Xavier monitored Issei's consciousness as it healed. Wakanda provided a secure facility for recovery. The Ravagers kept watch, making sure no secondary threats took advantage of the situation.

And the wives never left his side.

They rotated through shifts, making sure someone was always present when he woke from fever dreams, always there when the pain became unbearable, always reminding him why he had pushed himself that far: because they were worth it.

"The Theatre has stopped recording," Rocket mentioned to Yondu as they sat watching the observation deck from a distance. "Did you notice? No more HUD. No more notifications. No more gacha pulls since the battle."

"Maybe it's taking a break," Yondu's whistle was thoughtful. "Even a cosmic archive needs to process what happened. We just fought *reality itself* and won. That's... not nothing."

"I think it's waiting," Rocket said. "I think we proved something, and now the Theatre is waiting to see what we prove next. What happens when the hero who nearly burned himself out completely has to rebuild everything from nothing."
