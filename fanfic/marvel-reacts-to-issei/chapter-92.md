## THE ACCOUNTING

The stage transforms. No longer a single frame or a memory. Now it is a vast ledger, scrolling endlessly, displaying numbers that have no meaning because their scale exceeds comprehension.

A Watcher speaks. Not *the* Watcher, but one of a collective of thousands, entities that exist to observe and record all timelines simultaneously. This one's voice carries the exhaustion of witnessing something that shouldn't exist.

**"WE HAVE A PROBLEM WITH OUR RECORDS."**

In the TVA's section, bureaucratic panic spreads like fire. The Time Variance Authority prides itself on perfect temporal accounting. Every timeline is tracked, catalogued, classified by threat potential. That there could be timelines outside their knowledge is impossible. That there are **disappearing** timelines is more than impossible—it is a classification error so severe it suggests their entire infrastructure requires revision.

Kang stands, his temporal energy crackling with agitation. "What do you mean, 'disappearing'?" His voice carries the edge of someone who has conquered time itself and finds the concept of control slipping. "Timelines are fixed. They branch, they converge, they collapse—but they don't disappear. That violates the fundamental mathematics of temporal mechanics."

**"THEY DO NOT DISAPPEAR NATURALLY,"** the Watcher responds. **"THEY ARE BEING ERASED. AND THE ERASURES ARE NOT REGISTERED IN STANDARD TEMPORAL DATABASES."**

Doctor Strange stands in the mystic arts section, his third eye already open, attempting to perceive the problem through magical means. "How long has this been occurring?"

**"APPROXIMATELY NINE THOUSAND, TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE TIMELINES HAVE BEEN ERASED OVER THE PAST SEVENTY-FIVE YEARS. APPROXIMATELY THREE THOUSAND MORE IN THE PAST DECADE ALONE. THE RATE OF ERASURE IS ACCELERATING."**

The holographic display in the centre of the Theatre shifts, showing a three-dimensional map of timeline branches. It is beautiful and terrible—a cosmic tree with branches that suddenly end, cut off as if by shears, leaving phantom stumps where futures should continue.

## THE MEMORY BURDEN

Doctor Strange's third eye closes abruptly. His hand trembles. "You're saying," he begins carefully, "that these timelines were erased, and no one knows. No one remembers. Because the erasure is retroactive. Those timelines never existed from the perspective of anyone except—"

**"EXCEPT ISSEI HYOUDOU AND THE THEATRE. YES. THE THEATRE RETAINS FRAGMENTS OF ERASED TIMELINES AS REFERENCE DATA. ISSEI HYOUDOU RETAINS MEMORIES THAT EXIST NOWHERE ELSE IN CREATION."**

Wong, practical and grounded despite his mastery of mysticism, asks the logical question: "Why does Issei retain the memories? Erasure should eliminate all traces."

**"BECAUSE ISSEI ERASED THE TIMELINES HIMSELF. THE ERASURE WAS NOT EXTERNAL. IT WAS AN ACT OF WILL. AND THE MEMORIES OF ERASING SOMETHING REMAIN IN THE MIND OF THE ENTITY THAT PERFORMED THE ERASURE. TO FORGET WOULD REQUIRE HIM TO ERASE HIMSELF. WHICH HE CANNOT DO ENTIRELY WITHOUT CEASING TO EXIST."**

In the Watcher section, the Watcher-collective begins to confer among themselves. Their communication is silent, across dimensional frequencies human perception cannot access. But the disturbance in their collective presence is clear: they are agitated. They are afraid.

One Watcher speaks again, its voice carrying the weight of entities who have seen civilizations rise and fall, and have never been moved to speak until now.

**"WE HAVE A CONFESSION TO MAKE. THE WATCHERS DO NOT INTERVENE IN CONFLICTS. WE OBSERVE. WE RECORD. WE MAINTAIN. BUT WE DO NOT ACT. HOWEVER..."**

**"WE HAVE OBSERVED TIMELINES BEING ERASED THAT WE HAD CLASSIFIED AS IMPORTANT. TIMELINES THAT WERE SUPPOSED TO PRODUCE SIGNIFICANT MULTIVERSAL CONSEQUENCES. TIMELINES THAT WOULD HAVE ALTERED THE BALANCE OF POWER ACROSS INFINITY."**

**"AND WE DID NOT ATTEMPT TO STOP THE ERASURES BECAUSE, AT THE MOMENT OF EACH ERASURE, WE LACKED THE KNOWLEDGE THAT THEY WERE OCCURRING. OUR RECORDS SHOW NO INTERFERENCE. OUR ARCHIVES SHOW NO THREAT. BECAUSE ERASURE, WHEN PERFORMED AT THIS SCALE, REMOVES ITSELF FROM THE CAUSAL CHAIN. IT BECOMES RETROACTIVELY NON-EVENT."**

Strange's face goes very still. "How many people know this is happening?"

**"BEFORE THIS MOMENT: THREE ENTITIES. ISSEI HYOUDOU, WHO PERFORMED THE ERASURES. THE THEATRE, WHICH RETAINS FRAGMENTS. AND ONE OTHER, WHO KNEW AND CHOSE TO LET EVENTS PROCEED."**

**"AFTER THIS MOMENT: EVERYONE IN THE THEATRE."**

## THE UNSPOKEN QUESTION

Thanos rises, and the cosmic weight of his presence seems to darken the space around him. He has conquered half the universe. He has made impossible choices. He has destroyed civilizations without flinching. But even he seems diminished by this revelation.

"What did he see that was worth erasing all of these timelines?" Thanos asks. It is not a question of judgment. It is a question of recognition—one terrible being acknowledging another who has made choices that exceed normal morality.

**"IN ERASED TIMELINE 1,847: A FUTURE WHERE TRIHEXA WON. WHERE THE BEAST'S APOCALYPSE CONSUMED THE ENTIRETY OF THAT BRANCH AND CASCADED INTO ADJACENT BRANCHES. WHERE TWELVE BILLION LIVES BECAME VECTORS FOR THE PROTO-DEVOURER'S EMERGENCE."**

**"IN ERASED TIMELINE 2,104: A FUTURE WHERE ISSEI HYOUDOU DIED. WHERE HIS POWER PASSED TO ANOTHER, LESS CONTROLLED INDIVIDUAL. WHERE THAT INDIVIDUAL MADE CHOICES THAT SHATTERED REALITY AT A FUNDAMENTAL LEVEL."**

**"IN ERASED TIMELINE 3,247: A FUTURE WHERE RIAS GREMORY AND ISSEI'S WIVES WERE ERASED BY THE SAME DEVOURER ENTITY. WHERE ISSEI, IN HIS GRIEF, BECAME AN INSTRUMENT OF DESTRUCTION SO COMPLETE THAT HE CONSUMED CIVILIZATIONS TRYING TO ANNIHILATE THE ENTITY THAT HAD TAKEN THEM."**

**"ACROSS NINE THOUSAND, TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE ERASED TIMELINES, THE PATTERN IS THE SAME: FUTURES THAT LEAD INEVITABLY TO LARGER CATASTROPHES. FUTURES THAT MUST NEVER OCCUR BECAUSE THEIR OCCURRENCE WOULD PROPAGATE INTO INFINITE DAMAGE."**

**"ISSEI DID NOT ERASE THESE TIMELINES OUT OF CRUELTY. HE ERASED THEM OUT OF MERCY."**

In the Marvel section, Black Panther has stood and is pacing. "Then what is the actual enemy? What is this Devourer thing?"

The Watcher does not answer. The silence is answer enough.

Kang finally understands. "It's not in any of the timelines. It exists in the space between them. In the gaps left by erasures. It feeds on what was never allowed to happen."

**"CORRECT. THE PROTO-DEVOURER EMERGES FROM THE PROBABILITY SPACE WHERE ERASED TIMELINES WOULD HAVE CONTINUED. IT BUILDS ITSELF FROM ABANDONED POSSIBILITIES. FROM FUTURES THAT NEVER WERE ALLOWED TO FLOURISH. AND EACH TIME A TIMELINE IS ERASED, IT GROWS STRONGER, BECAUSE IT CONSUMES THE ABSENCE THAT ERASURE CREATES."**

Strange's comprehension is immediate and terrible. "So every time Issei erased a timeline to stop the Devourer from emerging—"

**"HE FED IT. HE MADE IT STRONGER. HE ACCELERATED THE TIMELINE OF ITS INEVITABLE MANIFESTATION. THIS IS WHAT THE THEATRE CALLS TRAGEDY IN ITS TRUEST FORM: THE ACT COMMITTED TO PREVENT A DISASTER BECOMES THE CAUSE OF THAT DISASTER."**

## THEATRE LEDGER — SESSION 92

### **Entry One: The Catalogue of Loss**
Nine thousand two hundred and forty-three timelines exist only in memory now, and only three entities retain those memories: Issei, the Theatre, and one unknown observer who permitted this cascade to continue. Each erased timeline contained billions of lives, civilizations, histories that spanned epochs. They are not lost because they were never recorded—the Watchers attempted to record, but erasure removes itself retroactively from the recording medium. They are lost because they were unmade. And the only record is in the mind of a man who should not have been required to carry such weight.

### **Entry Two: The Paradox of Mercy**
Issei's greatest act of compassion—erasing timelines to prevent their greater suffering—has become his greatest tragedy. For each timeline he destroyed to prevent the Devourer's emergence, he fed the Devourer, accelerated its manifestation, made its inevitable emergence more powerful. This is the cruel mathematics of multiversal mechanics: sometimes the correct choice and the wrong choice are identical. Sometimes mercy and catastrophe wear the same face. Issei faces a problem with no solution because the problem exists in the space between possibilities, and all efforts to eliminate it from one branch feed it in another.

### **Entry Three: The Watcher's Confession**
The Watchers do not interfere. This is foundational to their nature. But in observing Issei's erasures without attempting to stop them, they became complicit in his tragedy. They chose to allow a single man to carry the weight of nine thousand timelines because to stop him would have been interference. This is not judgment of the Watchers—they are bound by their nature. But it is an acknowledgment that sometimes, in the service of maintaining principle, we permit catastrophe to unfold. The Watchers now break their silence because silence has become impossible.

### **Entry Four: The True Enemy**
The entity that emerges from erasure is not Trihexa, not Apophis, not any threat Issei has faced before. It exists in the gaps between timelines, built from abandoned possibilities and forgotten futures. It cannot be fought with conventional power because it does not exist in conventional space. It cannot be negotiated with because it does not comprehend compromise—only the certainty that existence owes a debt to all possible versions of itself that were erased. Issei's enemies have always been things he could strike, things he could destroy or seal. This enemy is made of what was never allowed to be. And therein lies the fundamental problem: how do you defeat something that exists in what never happened?
